- **A plan has limits, and says them** (0241, 0242, `docs/plans.md`). Every plan has every feature; **Starter** carries the numbers and **Pro** and **Studio Max** are unlimited. `plans.limits` keys: `projects`, `invoices` (in the plan year), `leads` (held now), `team_logins`, `team_members` (no login), `enquiry_forms`, `facebook_pages`, `packages` (not the sample), `storage_mb`; a missing key is unlimited. **The plan year follows the billing date, never the calendar** (`companies.plan_period_start`, `plan_window()`): pay on 15 Oct and the counts start again on 15 Oct; a monthly plan counts the same yearly numbers, never a monthly share (0267, owner 17 Oct: "you cannot bar that by month"): Starter, Diamond or regular, monthly or yearly, is **30 projects and 30 invoices a year** (all usable in one month), **30 leads at a time**, **3 team logins and 10 crew**, and its cards say exactly that. The database stops the next one being made (`enforce_plan_limit`, 54000 → 402 in words → a toast with **Upgrade**); nothing made is touched, and a trial and an unlimited plan are never stopped. **Starter holds 30 leads at a time, from every source** (owner, 29 Oct; 0289): the count is leads not archived and not merged, never a yearly tally; typed, imported, form, QR, Facebook and WhatsApp leads all stop at it, and archiving or deleting one makes room (bringing one back counts again). A refused Facebook lead stays in the import log for Retry, the form tells the person to call the studio (402), and the owner's bell says so once a day (`plan_full_notice`). A check that must run before an outside call (a Facebook Page subscribe) uses `plan_room()`. Upgrading part-way costs only the difference (`plan_quote`, `payment_orders.kind`/`credit`) and starts a new plan year; a lower plan waits for the current one to end. **The pricing page stays short** (owner: "easy on the eyes"): `PlanPicker` -- a Pay yearly / monthly switch, three cards of at most three lines with the price a day, one row of "Every plan has every feature" chips, and "Compare plans" (closed until pressed) in sections -- What you can make, Your team, Getting leads, **Photo sharing (Frameline gallery)** with the Drive photos each plan includes, In every plan, Extras -- at most six rows each (`COMPARE_GROUPS` in `plan-features.ts`, numbers read from the plan rows; owner, 17 Nov: "added features rows and columns so users know what they get in each plan and at what are the Frameline limits"). Settings → Subscription shows one bar per limit ("12 of 30 projects this year", "Counts start again on 15 Oct 2027"). `plans.includes` (white_label, whatsapp_api, sequences_auto) is Studio Max's; `company_can()` reads it. Starter / Pro / Studio Max are **on sale since 0256** (owner, 10 Oct, after the preview). **Buying a plan is a conversation for now**: every plan button is a green **Talk to us on WhatsApp** (`useTalkToUs`, `planEnquiryMessage`: plan, how it is paid, price + GST, studio, login, "at the IPC Diamond member price" for a member, "please call me") to the Help console's number or ours, 91 91091 92065 (`SALES_WHATSAPP`) -- never an email button -- and the platform owner gives the plan in Studio Access Manager. **Give plan does the upgrade maths** (owner, 17 Nov; 0306): picking a plan shows `plan_quote`'s sentence and the amount to charge (`GET /platform/studios/:id/plan-quote`), and an upgrade or a lower plan offers **Start today** (an upgrade charges the difference, the days left come off) or **After the current plan** (full price, the days left kept), each with its end date; `platform_assign_plan(company, plan, start_today)`. **Compare plans** is a bordered primary button, easy to see. Razorpay checkout stays in the code, unused. **Platform → Plans** (`routes/platform/plans.tsx`, `platform_plans()`, `platform_set_plan_on_sale()`) lists every plan with its limits, the studios on it and an On sale switch; taking one off never touches a studio already on it. No card promises message credits until they are built. The WhatsApp setup guide is `/help/whatsapp`.

- **Refer a studio.** Settings → Money → **Refer a studio** gives the owner their link (`/?studio_ref=CODE`, `my_studio_ref_code()`, 0237) with Copy and WhatsApp Share, what a referral earns in a sentence ("being finalised" until set), and the studios that joined: Joined · on trial / On a paid plan / Rewarded ₹X / Not counted. `?studio_ref=` on any page is kept 60 days in the browser and sent with sign-up; `claim_studio_referral()` links the new studio (never itself, never twice, a wrong code ignored, sign-up never fails for it). "Paid" is read from the referred studio's first paid `payment_orders` row. The reward, the new studio's discount and the hold are the platform owner's, set on `/platform/studio-referrals` (`platform_settings.studio_ref_*`), where a paid referral is marked rewarded or not counted; no money moves on its own and `create_payment_order` is untouched.

Platform admins are connect@wpbmastery.in, connect@themulberryweddings.in, solankiharsh0217@gmail.com and nishagraham11@gmail.com (0219, 0246, 0287, 0291); another is added only on the owner's word, by a migration copied from 0291: its email goes in `platform_admin_emails`, an existing login is made one at once, and a later sign-up only once the inbox is proven (`claim_platform_admin`, called after the confirmation link and Google sign-in) -- never from the email alone, because a studio can give a team login any email and mark it confirmed. **A platform admin's own studio is open for life** (owner, 28 Oct: "why is there a trial system over here?"; 0288, `open_platform_admin_studios()`): any studio a platform admin owns or runs as super_admin has `grandfathered_until` at the end of 2099 -- no trial, no days left, no limits -- for admins added later and studios they make later too (triggers on `platform_admins` and `users`); the plan card and Plan & billing say "Open for life" (`isLifetime` in `plan-line.ts`).

- **The platform owner sees what each studio does, and can make some of them an offer** (owner, 17 Oct; 0268). Studio Access Manager shows, beside every studio, one activity word -- **Busy** (in on 3+ days this week and has made work) · **Trying it** · **Gone quiet** · **Never started** (`activityLevel` in `features/platform/activity.ts`) -- what it has made ("12 projects · 4 invoices · 30 leads · 5 team") and when it was last in, from `platform_studio_activity()` (counts only, never contents). Rows can be ticked: **Make an offer** (`OfferDialog`: 10/15/20/25% or own, which plans, last day, a line from the owner, "IPC Diamond members too" off by default) or **Call list** (CSV). **An offer is for a studio's first payment only, never a renewal, and for the regular price unless the offer says Diamond too** (owner): `platform_make_offers()` skips a studio that has bought before (`studio_has_bought`) or is Diamond, and the toast names who and why; a new offer for a studio ends its old one. The owner sees it as a gold strip on Home and Plan & billing (`OfferStrip`, a last day, never a timer) and the offer price on the cards it covers (`offerFor`); Talk to us on WhatsApp names it. Seen and asked are marked once (`offer_mark`); giving the studio a plan it covers marks it used (`platform_offer_used`). The **Offers** tab lists each one: Sent → Seen → Asked on WhatsApp → Paid. Nothing is charged here; the plan is still given in Studio Access Manager at the offer price.

- **More than one brand on one login, by plan** (owner, 20 Oct; 0276). Starter, Diamond or regular, and a trial run one studio; **Pro up to 3**; **Studio Max as many as they like** (`plans.limits.brands`: 1 / 3 / no key; `brand_limit()` falls back on the tier). A brand is a studio of its own (leads, projects, team, money), made by `create_brand(name)` the way 0159 makes a second studio for a login -- a new profile of the same identity owns it -- so it opens from **Switch studio** in the account menu. It rides on the first studio's plan: `companies.brand_of`, the plan, expiry and plan year copied on creation and kept in step by `companies_brands_follow_plan`; a brand cannot open brands of its own (the count is per first studio). The owner adds one from **Add a brand** in the account menu (`features/billing/brands.tsx`, `GET/POST /subscription/brands`, "1 of 3 brands"); on a plan with one studio the line says "With Pro (3) or Studio Max (no limit)" and opens Plan & billing. The cards say it (Pro: "Up to 3 brands on one login"; Studio Max: "Everything unlimited, as many brands as you like") and Compare plans has **Brands on one login**.
