# Team logins, staff screens, attendance, leave, daily tasks and pay details

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **Nobody is signed out unless they sign out.** A session slides: every refresh pushes it 90 days on (0221), so it ends only on Sign out, a password change, removal from the studio, or 90 days without opening the app. A lost refresh reply is not theft, and a tab that loses a refresh race gets 409 and picks up the winner's token -- never clear the session on anything but a 401/403 from `/auth/refresh`.

- **Team logins need no email at all.** Any email, even a made-up one, is only a username: the team signs in with it and the password the studio gave them, and nothing on the team side ever asks them to confirm an email (`session.email_verified` is true for everyone but the owner; the confirm strip is the owner's alone). The owner or an admin sets a person's email and password from **Sign-in details** (Team row menu, member page; `POST /team/members/:id/sign-in`, `features/team/SignInDetailsDialog.tsx`) -- it also gives a no-login member a login and fixes a mistyped email, signs them out everywhere, and is refused for a login that also opens another studio. Everyone changes their own password on My profile. A removed or switched-off person is told "Your sign-in for {studio} has been turned off", never "wrong password".

- **Staff see their own work, not the studio's.** Whoever cannot edit projects and has no billing or money access (`seesStudioWork` in `packages/permissions`) gets only the projects they work on (no money, no client phone), their own shoots, data and activity; the studio's screens answer 403 (`requireStudioWork` / `worksOn` in `services/api/src/lib/scope.ts`) and their pages send staff to their own version (`AuthedPage studioWork=…`; a `?d=` notification link opens on My work). A new studio-wide endpoint uses `requireStudioWork`; a new studio-wide page passes `studioWork` and its menu line `studioWork: true`. Owners widen a person through Manage access (Project Manager, billing).

- **A team member's Home is their day** (`features/dashboard/StaffHome.tsx`, rules in `today.ts`): the attendance card, then **Today** -- one list in time order, late first, each line with its one action (shoot: Confirm / I've reached; task: Start / Submit; edit: I've started; cards: Hand over; a CRM call given to them: Call / Done via `/me/follow-ups`) -- then **Coming up** (7 days), one line "3 tasks · 2 edits → My work", and the profile line with **Later**. No tiles, no score, no quick links. A note or voice note waiting for the editor goes to the top of Today ("Asha sent you a voice note · 2 h ago") with **Play** and **Reply** right there (`last_note`, `unread_notes` on `MyDeliverable`; playing it or opening the item marks it heard, `POST /me/deliverables/:id/notes-read`); cards to hand over open the hand-over box on Home. Their menu is Home · My work · My shoots · Attendance & leave · My leads · My payouts · My performance (My Tasks folded into My work); the phone bar is at most five with Menu, and Attendance only while the studio has it on. An owner or manager who also shoots gets one **Your day** line above the command centre.

- **Attendance is off until the owner turns it on** (Settings → Attendance, `set_attendance_policy`, 0224). Off: nobody is marked, swept absent, reminded or cut a rupee; `enabled_at` is the first day anything counts. On: one place (use my location, or paste a Google Maps link -- `POST /hr/places/resolve-link`) with a radius in chips, working hours (start, grace, end, "half day if less than N h", weekly off, optional "N late marks = ½ day"), and an optional selfie (`<input capture="user">`, shrunk to 480 px; only the person and managers see it; purged after 60 days). A GPS fix rougher than 500 m is refused and a rough one gets the benefit of its doubt. **A shoot day counts through "I've reached"** (source `shoot`) and is never absent or cut; an office tap never overwrites it. The owner reads it on Attendance → **Today** (one sentence, then Not in yet / Late / In / On a shoot / On leave) and **Month** (P L H A S Lv Off Hol, totals worked out like payroll, CSV). Copy any attendance function from 0224.

- **A freelancer is paid when their cards come in.** The data dialog (`DataRecordDialog`) carries "{name}'s payout ₹__ · Pay later / Paid ₹X / Part paid" for anyone with `team_payouts`, prefilled from the booking and changeable there; "Record everyone's cards" has a Pay tick per person. Both save through `POST /team-payouts/slot/:id/pay` (a changed amount becomes final, then the payment goes into `team_slot_settlements`). Each project's payouts are Finance → **Payouts** (`tabs/PayoutsTab.tsx`, "Cards in" chip, Pay); every payout and payment is Team → Pay → **Team payouts** (Shoot payouts first, opening on **Owed now**, project filter, CSV); the person sees their own on **My payouts** (`/payouts/my`, `GET /me/payouts`).

- **Whoever pays the team can fill in where the pay goes.** The Pay to card in every pay dialog (`features/team/PayToCard.tsx`) has **Add UPI or bank** and **Edit** (`PUT /team/members/:id/pay-to`, same checks as the person's own form; owner, salary or payout editors only, and a delegate never changes the owner's). The audit names the fields that changed, never their values; a person who can sign in gets "Your payment details were changed" in their bell, so pay can never be quietly redirected. The card sits inside the Pay dialog's form, so it is a div with its own Save, never a nested form.

- **Leave has a balance.** Leave → Balances (owners and managers) sets the days a year per kind (casual, sick, paid; `leave_allowances`, 0228) and lists everyone as "9 of 12 casual left"; a person sees their own balance on My leave and in Ask for leave. Approvals say "Has 2 days casual left · this is 3 days, 1 over" and offer **Approve, 1 day unpaid**: the days still covered stay as asked and the rest become an approved unpaid request, which payroll already cuts. Days off inside a leave are not counted; a half day is 0.5.

- **A team member's project page is their work, the days and the data** (`routes/my-work/project/$projectId.tsx`, `GET /me/projects/:id`): My work · Shoots & team (each day, venue, who shot it, "(you)") · Data (main disk + folder, backup, link, with Copy, who copied it). Never money, the client's phone or anyone's pay. My work lists edits in sections (Changes asked · Late · This week · Later · Waiting for review · Done lately) with the shoots each comes from, "Data ready · WD Red 03 · /folder" or "Waiting for Haldi data", and I've started · Hand in work · Open project. Due dates read "4 days left" / "Due tomorrow" / "2 days late" everywhere (`shared/ui/days-left.ts`).

- **A login never opens on an expired studio while another of its studios is open** (0269, `pick_login_profile`), and **Find a login** in Studio Access Manager (`FindLoginDialog`, `platform_login_lookup`, `platform_open_first`) shows every studio an email's login opens, which comes first, and gives 30 days or sets the one to open first. **Open items for the owner and for Claude are in `docs/owner-todo.md`** -- read it when the owner asks what is pending, and keep it current.

- **Daily tasks come back by themselves, and crew money is one tap from Money** (owner, 14 Nov; 0305). **Daily tasks** is a view of Tasks (`?view=daily`, a **Daily tasks** button in its header; `features/tasks/DailyTasks.tsx`, `routines.ts`): **Add daily task** sets what, **who does it** (several people, the pick-many look), **which days** (Every day · Mon–Sat · Pick days · Once a month) and **Finish by** (End of the day = 7 pm, or a time), and each card says today per person -- Done today · Still to do · Not done, late -- with the last 7 days. `task_routines` + `task_routine_people`; each person gets their own copy each India day as an ordinary task (`tasks.routine_id`, `routine_for`, tag Daily), made by `generate_routine_tasks()` on the five-minute messages tick and at once when a routine is saved, quietly (`ipc.task_quiet`: no "New task" every morning). A copy still open past its time rings the person and whoever set it, once (`routine_tasks_remind()`); next day an open copy is closed as missed (`routine_missed`). The person ticks it on their Home in one tap (**Done**, `routine_task_done()`, `POST /tasks/daily/:id/done`; no review). Routes in `modules/tasks/routes-routines.ts`. **Crew payouts** is a tab of Money (`/money/crew-payouts`, the Team payouts page at its own address) with a **By project** view (`crewByProject`: crew payouts · paid · owed now · after the shoot, and Pay into the project's Payouts); the project's Cost sheet says paid per person (Paid ₹X / ₹X of ₹Y / Not paid yet) and carries **Pay the team** into Payouts. A project's sub-tab row sits in its own tinted panel named for its group, chips with a firm border, the open one filled (owner: they were "not visible at all"). A restored page is always asked for again (`restoreCache` marks restored queries stale), so a refresh shows another person's change.
