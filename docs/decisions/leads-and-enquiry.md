# Leads, follow-ups, enquiry forms, partners and wedding QR codes

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **Facebook lead ads can always be reset.** The card (`features/facebook/MetaConnectionCard.tsx`) shows **Disconnect Facebook** whenever any Page or token is held: every subscribed Page is unsubscribed from leadgen (best effort), then `meta_forget_studio()` (0230) removes all Page tokens and Pages -- never leads or the import log. On opening, `POST /meta/check` asks Facebook whether a held token still works; a 190 / "not authorized" (`isMetaAuthError`) forgets everything and the card says "Your Facebook connection expired. Please reconnect." Connect with Facebook carries `auth_type=rerequest` and the five scopes (pages_show_list, pages_manage_metadata, pages_read_engagement, business_management, leads_retrieval); a Facebook login lists only the Pages picked in its dialog. No user token is ever stored; tokens are never logged.

- **A lead is booked or lost from the top of its drawer.** "Book it" (primary) opens Book {name}: the price starts from the lead's Budget with "This is the booked price, not the quote.", and booking lands on the project's quotation. "Lost" asks for a reason. A booked lead reads "Booked — {project}" (green, a link). The header carries the next follow-up ("Follow up 3 Oct, 11:00 am"; amber "No follow-up yet" or once it is past), the Timeline says "First contact in 2 h" (the same stamp as Reports > Team), and Leads shows an amber "3 not contacted yet" that opens that view.

- **A studio builds its own enquiry form** (`/enquiry-forms`, 0240): **Your website** or **A vendor** (QR). **Edit form** opens the builder with the form beside it (`FormBuilderDialog`, drawn by the same `EnquiryFormView` as the public page): heading, a line under it, each field Off · Ask · Required (name and phone always), button colour from the seven tones, logo on/off, the after-send words. The database holds the rule (`enquiry_form_submit` refuses a missing required field with 422 and never writes a field the form does not ask). A website form gives **Copy code**: an iframe of `/enquire/<code>?embed=1` that tells the page its height (`enquiryEmbedCode`); `/enquire/*` alone may be framed (`apps/web/public/_headers`). A website form never shows the vendor badge.

- **The enquiry form follows Wedding Yantra's hand-off spec** (owner, 19 Oct: "where their version differs from this file, this file wins"; `docs/handoff/enquiry-form-qr-whatsapp-spec.md`, 0274) -- its screens, words and rules, in Studio AutoPilot's own brand (navy and gold, the seven tones, the studio's font), never Wedding Yantra's saffron palette or fonts. Each studio has **one main form** (`enquiry_forms.is_main`, made on first open by `enquiry_main_form()`) at `/f/<slug>`; `/enquire/<code>` and every QR printed before still open. **Enquiry form & QR codes** is `/follow-ups/form`, one tap from Leads (**Form & QR** in its header) and from Settings → Forms. It is short (owner, 20 Oct: the long page was "overwhelming"): the header carries the on/off switch and **Add partner**, then four chips, one shown at a time (`?tab=`): **Your QR code** (with the website code and the leads download), **Partners**, **After they send**, **Questions** -- no explainer lines, and staff see only the QR. Its parts: **Your QR code** (the print card as it will look, Download for print (HD) · SVG, the link with WhatsApp · Copy link · Open form), **After they send the form** (the main form's, which **every partner QR follows until it has its own**: a partner's page carries **After they send, through <name>'s QR** with **Same as your main form** first, then its own chat (number and first message, empty ones fall back to the form's, then the studio's), thank-you words or link -- owner, 25 Oct: "sometimes we need to send some unique links to different QR codes"; `enquiry_partners.after_submit` null = same, 0281, `PartnerAfterSendCard`; the partner row shows Own link / Own chat / Own thank-you; **a partner QR also carries what it is for and its own questions** (owner, 25 Oct: "whatever customization we add to the particular QR code ... has to come into the CRM"): **What is this QR for?** (`enquiry_partners.occasion`, "Priya & Rahul's wedding", shown on the form and kept as `crm_leads.enquiry_occasion`) and up to ten questions (`enquiry_partners.questions`, a typed answer or one pick from a list, optional or **Must answer**, `PartnerQuestions` in the partner dialog, checked by `enquiry_questions_ok`), asked after the form's own; the answers are kept on the lead with each question's words (`crm_leads.enquiry_answers`), go in the WhatsApp message and a repeat enquiry's note, and the lead drawer shows **For <occasion>** under the partner chip and an **Answers from <partner>'s QR** card under Events (`drawer/FormAnswersCard.tsx`); WhatsApp, the default: the client lands in a wa.me chat with "Hi <studio>! I just sent you an enquiry." and their name, event, city, what they need, guests; the number is the form's, else `companies.invoice_phone`, else the owner's -- or a plain thank-you, or an https:// link, the database refuses anything else), **Questions on your form** (name and mobile always; email, event, event date, city, what they need -- the studio's own `enquiry_service` list with + Add -- guests, budget, anything else; a new form asks email, event, date and anything else), and **a QR for each partner or vendor** (`enquiry_partners`: `?p=<code>` credits the lead, `crm_leads.partner_id`, and the lead reads **via <partner>** -- a teal `PartnerChip` -- on its pipeline card, in the list and at the top of its drawer (a link to the partner), with a **partners** filter on Leads (owner, 20 Oct: "otherwise there will be a lot of leads and we'll not be able to track that"); a private page at `/enquiry-view/<token>` with masked numbers and New / In talks / Booked / Not booked, never money; **Make QR** on a vendor, and on the vendor's own dialog). 0195's vendor forms were moved over as partners with the same code and page link. **The same number while its lead is open never makes a second lead**: a note "Enquired again" goes on it and whoever has it is told (a deliberate difference from the spec's one-day window, because a number always finds its lead here); a new lead tells the owner "New enquiry: <name>" with "Wedding · 14 Feb 2027 · Jaipur · via <partner>". 10 sends an hour per address. **Excel / CSV** of leads (`/leads-export`, CRM Export only; sixteen columns, bold frozen header with filters, UTF-8 CSV that never runs a formula): **Download leads** in the Leads header (every lead), and a green `LeadsDownloadStrip` **on top** of the hub's Partners, All partners and each partner's page (owner, 21 Oct: "it has to come in the All Partners section ... shown on top"); every download is **Google Sheets · Excel · CSV** (`DownloadButtons`; Google Sheets saves the Excel and opens a new sheet with "File → Import → Upload" in the toast, because we hold no Google sign-in). Staff see the link and QR only. The menu stays as it is: no Leads sub-menu.

- **A lead can start with just a name.** Add lead needs a name or a phone (`createLeadRequest` refuses neither); a lead with no number shows an amber dashed "Add their number" in the drawer header, and **Book it asks for the number in its own dialog** (audit, 5 Nov) -- the API saves it on the lead before booking, and the lead's city goes to the new client. Same number still finds the existing lead.

- **Today's calls is one list.** A promised follow-up rides on its lead's call row (Done / Tomorrow there, `FollowUpOnRow.tsx`); only follow-ups whose lead is not owed a call today follow under Coming up (`mergeFollowUps` in `features/crm/follow-ups.ts`). Never a second list of the same people above the queue.

- **Forms & QR clean-up** (owner, 20 Oct audit; 0278). A number that enquires again through a partner's QR while its lead is open stays one lead but is credited to that partner when nobody had it; archived leads are left out of a partner's page, the partner counts and the main form's numbers (`enquiry_form_stats` also leaves out partner leads); a refresh of the form in the same visit is not another scan (`?seen=1` → `enquiry_public(key, p, p_count)`). Your QR code shows "42 scans · 9 enquiries · 2 booked from this QR and link"; each partner row is one sentence (`partnerLine`: "12 scans · 4 enquiries · 1 booked (25%) · last one 3 days ago · saw their page yesterday") and the best by bookings carries a gold **Top** chip (`topPartnerId`); a partner's QR card has **Send on WhatsApp** (`partnerQrText`). Switching the form off, **End partnership** and **Stop sharing** each ask first. "Just say thank you" edits the real thank-you words in place. The public form says "Too many tries" (429) or "Something went wrong" apart from "This form isn't available" (404), and Send puts the cursor on the first answer to fix.

- **A partner sees their leads' real details and can download them** (owner, 20 Oct; 0276). `/enquiry-view/<token>` shows each lead's full number (tap to call), email and city, and **Download all N leads (Excel / CSV)** -- made in the browser, mobile as ten digits, no cell runs as a formula. `enquiry_partners.show_phone` is on by default for every partner ("Show full contact details" on the studio's partner page hides them for one partner). The studio's own partner page lists each lead with phone and email.

- **A lead is given to one person, and only they are told** (owner, 25 Oct; 0282). The drawer opens with **Given to <name>** (`features/crm/AssigneeChip.tsx`, amber dashed "Give to someone" while nobody has it); Add lead, the bulk bar's Give to… and Setup's hand-over set it too. Whoever is given a lead gets "New lead for you: <name>" in their bell (`crm_leads_assigned_notify`; never for giving it to yourself, and a lead that arrives by itself is announced by the new-lead workflow), and moving a lead moves the old person's open follow-ups with it. **A team member works their leads on My leads** (`/my-leads`, staff menu line and a line on their Home; `GET /me/leads`, `/me/leads/:id`, notes and follow-ups through `/me`, the caller's own leads only): To do · All open · Booked & lost, Call and WhatsApp on each row, and in the panel the next follow-up (type, Today/Tomorrow/In 3 days/Next week or a time, Tomorrow and Done), a note box, what they told the QR and what happened. A lead link for someone without the CRM opens on My leads.

- **Every follow-up is on one board, and rings one person** (owner, 25 Oct). **Follow-ups** in the Leads header (amber "2 late") opens `/follow-ups/board` (`routes/follow-ups/board.tsx`, `features/crm/follow-up-board.ts`): Overdue · Today · Tomorrow · This week · Later columns, each card with the person, type, priority, time and note, and Call · WhatsApp · Tomorrow · Done; Everyone / Mine for whoever runs the studio, a search, and people, type and priority behind Filter. A follow-up is whose it was given to, else the lead's owner, else whoever wrote it (`whose_id` on every activity), and only that person is reminded: a bell alert 15 minutes before (`crm_follow_up_soon()`, on the five-minute messages tick) and an on-screen reminder that stays until they act on it, with Call and Open (`FollowUpReminders`, for staff without the CRM too, from `/me/follow-ups`).

- **The Leads header is minimal** (owner, 10 Nov: "a lot of buttons ... only the relevant ones, yet all the things present"): Today's calls | Follow-ups (with "2 late"), joined as one control · **Enquiry form & QR** (solid teal with its QR tile, so it is noticed) · **More** (Reports, Setup, Import leads, Download leads -- `MoreLeadTools` in `routes/follow-ups.tsx`) · **Add lead**. A new lead tool joins More, never another button in the row; the pipeline line under the toolbar says only the count and the money.

- **Wedding QR codes are not partners** (owner, 10 Nov; 0299). A partner is a shop or vendor that keeps the studio's flex at its counter; a **wedding QR** is one the studio prints and places at a client's wedding so guests who liked the work can enquire. Same machinery (`enquiry_partners` with `kind = 'wedding'` and its `project_id`, one per project, `create_wedding_qr()`), its own place: **Wedding QR codes** right after Your QR code on Enquiry form & QR codes (`?tab=wedding`, `features/enquiry-forms/WeddingQr.tsx`): **made like the studio's own QR, never asking for a project** (owner, 19 Nov; 0313): **Add wedding QR** is one box, the couple's names ("Priya & Rahul's wedding"), and **Make wedding QR** (`create_wedding_qr_named`; the same name gives that one back); only the project page's Make wedding QR ties one to its project. Its guests' form asks **only name, mobile, email and "Do you have a wedding in the coming months?"** (Yes / No), and on Yes **when and where** (`enquiry_wedding_fields`, `wedding` on the public form, `wedding_soon` on the send); the answer is kept on the lead (`enquiry_answers`) and Yes makes its event Wedding. Wedding QRs have their own **After they send** (a chip beside **Wedding QRs** in that tab, `WeddingAfterSendCard`, `PUT /enquiry-forms/:id/wedding-after`, `enquiry_forms.wedding_*`), "Same as your main form" until changed. Each row one sentence of scans · enquiries · booked with **Download for print (HD)** ("Loved our work? Scan to book us for your wedding"), Edit and Delete (asks first). Partners, All partners and their counts list `kind = 'partner'` only (`GET /enquiry-partners` defaults to it; `?kind=wedding|all`). A lead from one reads "via Priya & Rahul's Wedding QR" with a heart (`PartnerChip kind`), and the project's Referrals & wishes carries one line, "Wedding QR: 42 scans · 6 enquiries" or **Make wedding QR**.
