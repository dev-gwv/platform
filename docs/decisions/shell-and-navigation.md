# Menu, top bar, Home and app-wide screens

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **The menu is fourteen lines** (`shared/layout/nav.ts`): Home · Leads · Clients · Projects · Shoots · Post-Production · **Photo sharing** · Tasks · Data & Backup · Money · Team · Reports · **AI** · Settings (owner, 9 Nov: AI joined as its own line; its tools are cards on `/ai`, never more menu lines). **Photo sharing is the gallery's own line, right under Post-Production** (owner, 11 Nov: "it is too underrated ... it should come up as a photo sharing app on the left sidebar"; this replaces 5 Nov's "never a menu line"): it opens `/galleries`, the **Photo sharing app** (`routes/galleries.tsx`): a navy-to-teal band ("Share every wedding's photos", the studio's galleries · photos · visits, **Create a gallery** for any project via `ProjectPicker`, **Open the studio app** → `/galleries/app`, How it works), then **Galleries** (a card each: photos, visits, PIN, closing date, Open gallery · WhatsApp · Copy link and PIN · QR) and **Google Drive** (`DriveCard` for the owner; Starter sees the Import from Google Drive lock with Upgrade, from `galleryList.drive_import`). Its "New" stays until the first gallery (`galleryApp`). The solid teal **Photo gallery** button on the Post-Production page and the project's Gallery tab (now with **See all galleries**) stay. And for the owner alone one more, **Plan & billing**, just above Settings (`PLAN_BILLING` in `hubs.ts`; the owner: "the plans and the billings should be clearly visible on the left"). **Leads always opens on Pipeline** (List or Board holds only while you stay on the page, never remembered) and **Post-Production opens on People** (`?view=stages|list|tasks` still opens its own). Money is one line with a tab row (Payments · Invoices · Expenses · Profit & Loss, `MONEY` in `hubs.ts`); Projects has a **Needs attention** tab (Project Tracking, `PROJECTS_HUB`). There is no "More" group: alerts are the bell, reminders are a view of Tasks (`/tasks?view=reminders`; `/reminders` redirects), and Activity, Enquiry forms, Lead sources and Referrals are in Settings. Making something is the top bar's **+ New** (`NewMenu.tsx`: Lead, Project, Payment received, Expense, each the dialog its own page uses). One shoots page: `/shoots` opens Team Booking, `/shoots/$id` opens the project's Shoots tab. Pay opens on Team payouts; Monthly salaries is Payroll's history (Older salary records, no second Generate). A new page joins a hub's tab row or Settings, never a new menu line.

- **What is due shows in the top bar** (`shared/layout/DueChip.tsx`, `GET /me/due`): "1 late" in red or "3 due soon" in amber, hidden when nothing is close; staff see their own, people who run the studio's work also see the studio's edits with who has each. A deliverable alert opens the item (`/my-work?d=`). On a phone, staff get Home · My work · My shoots · Attendance · Menu (Attendance only while it is on).

- **An open tab moves to the newest release by itself** (owner, 20 Oct: studios could not see a page already deployed). Nobody is signed out, so a tab or the desktop/Android app can run an old build for days; `shared/pwa/update.ts` reads the live index.html every 10 minutes and when the tab comes back into view, and once its main script changed, the next link tap is a full load and a tab in the background reloads. Never cache index.html.

- **The dashboard greets by the time of day** ("FRIDAY, 2 OCTOBER" over "Good evening, Asha.") and has **three tiles**, each a number, one sentence and the one place to act (`features/dashboard/tiles.ts`): **Leads to call** ("4 to call today · 2 not contacted yet" → Today's calls), **Shoots this week** ("1 still needs people" → Team Booking) and **To collect** ("₹1,20,000 due in 30 days · ₹40,000 overdue" → Payments received, from `GET /billing/due`, so it is the same number as that page). Below them only Needs attention (the 3 projects most at risk) and Who you owe, apart from the one onboarding card above the tiles. One onboarding card at a time: the setup videos, Getting started, then the guide.

- **Getting started on the dashboard** (`features/dashboard/GettingStartedCard.tsx`, `GET /settings/getting-started`): after setup, for a studio's first 60 days, five steps that tick themselves from the data -- logo and name, a saved package (a template that is not the sample), a first enquiry, a booking with shoot days, a quotation sent. The next one is amber with its one button; "2 of 5 done" and the bar move at once. It closes at 5/5, after 60 days, or on Close (per person, `users.hints.getting_started`). It never repeats setup's three steps.

- **Studio Max puts the studio's own name and logo top left** (white label): `Brand` in `AppShell` reads `useBranding()` and, when `enabled`, shows the studio's logo (or initial) and name instead of the Studio AutoPilot wordmark.

- **Something new reads "New" for a week** (owner, 31 Oct: "mark all the places it can be seen as new for 7 days"). `shared/ui/new-badge.tsx`: a release gets a last day in `NEW_UNTIL` (India dates) and `fresh: '<key>'` on the places it shows -- a project sub-tab (`PROJECT_TABS`, which also marks its group), a hub tab (`HubTab`), a settings tab (and its rail line) -- or a `<NewBadge feature=…>` beside a button or heading. It goes by itself after that day; delete the key once it has passed. **The gallery's "New" (`galleryDrive`, `galleryApp`, `UNTIL_USED`) is not dated: it stays until the studio makes its first gallery** (owner, 5 Nov: "keep it on until they completely explore it and start using it ... if they do not start using it, do not take it off"); `newFor` reads `GET /galleries` -- that is now Google Drive's (`galleryDrive`) alone. **Photo sharing's "New" is per person** (owner, 11 Nov: "put a new sign for all the people ... once they start using it, we should take it off ... it should be noticeable"): `galleryApp` is in `PER_PERSON`, kept on `users.hints.photo_sharing` (`shown` = visits to `/galleries`, `closed` = used), so it shows for everyone, whatever the studio's galleries, until that person creates, opens, shares on WhatsApp, copies or prints the QR of a gallery, opens the studio app, or has visited 5 times (`useMarkPhotoSharingUsed`, `useCountPhotoSharingVisit`, `photoSharingUsed`). Its sign is the **loud** one (`NewBadge loud`: solid gold, navy NEW, pulsing for a person's first 3 visits) on the menu line and on Post-Production's Photo gallery button, a gold dot (`NewDot`) on the icon when the sidebar is folded and on the phone's Menu.

- **Audit clean-up (21 Nov) changed these screens.** The quotation toolbar hides **Send to client ▾** until the quotation is sent (the journey bar carries it until then; it opens the same menu, See it as the client first). A lead's quality chip **hot** also marks it hot for the call queue -- there is no separate Mark hot button. On a project's Shoots tab a fully staffed day's Assign team is a small outline **Manage team**; a passed day with cards out leads with an amber **Record cards** and its "Data: …" line is plain text beside it; Save as preset, Delete and Add who this day needs sit in the card's ⋯. The four money tiles show only on Overview and Finance. Filters on Clients, Payments received, Post-Production and Tasks sit behind **Filter** like Leads and Projects. Team Booking's current month opens at today ("Show N earlier shoots"). Bulk add gives a login only when **Give a login** is ticked, and is one card per person on a phone. Suggest a feature lives in the account menu; the days-left chip hides while the sidebar's plan card shows.

- **Second clean-up pass (21 Nov).** **A WhatsApp link is built only by `waLink` / `waShare` / `waLinkOrShare`** in `packages/domain/src/whatsapp.ts` (leading 0, spaces, +91 and foreign codes handled; a number that cannot be a phone gives no link); ESLint refuses a literal `wa.me/` anywhere else. **`tools/tutorials/check-stale.mjs`** lists, in CI's log, every video whose filmed files (`tools/tutorials/screens.json`) changed since it was recorded (`recorded.json`); after re-recording a video, or confirming it still matches, run `node tools/tutorials/check-stale.mjs --update <key>`. Money pages: an invoice row names one next step (Send / Record / View) with the rest under ⋯; the invoice page has one Print or PDF; Expenses, Invoices filters sit behind Filter; P&L and Payroll are cards on a phone; the Money tab reads Crew payouts. Settings: Lists holds every list (System no longer repeats it), System's technical details are folded, the audit log is "Who changed what"; Company profile's account card is two lines.
