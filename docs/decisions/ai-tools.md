# Quotation Designer, AI writers and referral cards

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **The Quotation Designer is the first AI tool** (owner, 9 Nov; 0295; the owner's Canva quotation rebuilt). **AI** in the menu opens `/ai` (`routes/ai.tsx`): one card per tool, the Designer live and the rest "Coming soon" -- never a button that goes nowhere. `/ai/designer` lists the studio's designs; **New design** picks a project (or none) and a look, and `fillDesign` (`features/designer/fill.ts`) fills the pages before the editor opens: cover, About us, one **Event** page per shoot (its roles become "3 Shooters with Assistants" and "2 Candid Photographer" lines), **Deliverables** (what the client sees, "Total Investment: INR 85,000/-", the agreed payment plan), **Add-ons** (the extra deliverables with their prices), Thank you; the cover, About us and Thank you come from the studio's last design so its story and photos are written once. A design is one JSON document (`designDoc` in `packages/contracts/src/designs.ts`, `quotation_designs.doc`); a page is a kind with fields, and a **template is only a theme** (`TEMPLATES`: Ivory Classic -- the Canva look --, Midnight Gold, Blush Rose, Sage Editorial; colours and two Google fonts, changeable under **Look**), so switching never loses a word. The editor (`routes/ai-designer-edit.tsx`) types on the page itself (`Editable`, Enter adds a line), photos are the studio's own uploads (`PhotoFrame`: shrunk to 2000 px, public file, move and zoom; an empty frame says "Add your photo"), pages move, hide, duplicate and delete, and it saves by itself. **Download PDF** prints a full-bleed A4 copy portalled onto `<body>` (`.designer-print`, `@page designer`). **Every plan has it; Starter (regular or Diamond) makes 30 designs a plan year** (`plans.limits.designs`, `enforce_plan_limit('designs')`, a bar on Plan & billing); Pro, Studio Max and a trial are unlimited. Stock-photo sites are not reachable from here; sample photos come from the owner. **Step 2 (0296, owner 9 Nov: "complete the quotation maker completely" before any other AI tool):** **Send to client** makes one live link, `/d/<token>` (`quotation_design_links`, service-only, the token kept whole so the same link is copied again; **Stop sharing** deletes it and the old link is a 404), sent on WhatsApp or copied, with "Opened 3 times · last 2 Nov, 6:40 pm" (`design_link_opened`, one per ten minutes, never the studio's own signed-in people); the client's page (`routes/design-public.tsx`) shows every shown page sized to the phone and Download PDF. **Write it for me** (violet, beside the tagline, About us, an event's team, the deliverables, each add-on, Thank you and a testimonial; tone Warm · Premium · Fun · Hinglish and an optional line) goes through `POST /designs/write` → `lib/design-writer.ts` on the assistant's engine and token budget, counted in the studio's daily AI allowance (`assistant_log`), and never invents prices or numbers; without `AI_API_KEY` it says AI writing is not switched on. Six looks (Royal Maroon and Modern Mono added) and three more page kinds: **Packages** (up to three side by side, the middle one raised), **Our team** (round photos, name, role) and **Kind words** (a testimonial). A project's quotation page carries **Premium design**, which opens New design on that project (`/ai/designer?project=`). **Step 3 (0297, owner 9 Nov: "everything that is there in Canva", "the background is white, there is no contrast"):** the editor is **full screen and dark like Canva's** (`routes/ai-designer-edit.tsx`): a navy-to-violet top bar (Designs · Undo · Redo · Saved · the name · Save as template · Download PDF · **Send to client** · ⋯ Make a copy / Delete), a left rail **Templates · Uploads · Pages · Look** (`features/designer/SidePanel.tsx`; beside the pages from 1024 px, over them below), a **text bar** while a text has the cursor (`TextBar.tsx`: size, alignment, bold, italic, colour, Reset; kept per text in `page.data.styles`, drawn by `withTextStyle`), each page's **Page colour** (`page.data.background`), and a foot bar with **zoom** (− slider + %, Fit) and **Scroll / Grid view**. **Undo/redo** (`history.ts`, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z; quick changes are one step). **Photos**: drag from Uploads onto any frame, drop a file from the computer straight onto a frame, or tap a frame and then a photo; drag inside a frame to move what shows (`PhotoFrame`, `context.tsx`). **Every photo is made small before it uploads** (`shrink.ts`: over 1.5 MB or 2400 px becomes a JPEG under 1.5 MB, "made smaller, 28.4 MB → 1.3 MB"; a 66.8 MB test file went up at 1.41 MB), and kept in the studio's **Uploads** library (`design_photos`; removing one never touches a design that shows it). **Templates**: six ready-made designs (`starters.ts`: Classic Wedding, Royal Indian Wedding, Packages Menu, Pre-Wedding Shoot, Modern Minimal, Destination Wedding) and the studio's own (**Save as template**, `quotation_designs.is_template`, never counted against Starter's 30); New design picks one, a project is poured in (`pourProject`: its events, deliverables and add-ons take the template's places) and a ready-made one keeps the studio's story from its last design (`keepStudioPages`). The designs list has **Designs · Your templates**, each with open, Make a copy and Delete. While the editor is open, toasts rise from the bottom (the text bar sits where they drop in). A press on empty workspace closes the text bar and must never put the cursor in the nearest text (Chrome does; the canvas prevents it on mousedown). **Step 4 (owner, 9 Nov: "in Canva you can drag the photo directly by clicking on it, and rulers come on the canvas"):** every photo and text is a **Movable** (`features/designer/Movable.tsx`): **press and drag a photo** to move it anywhere on the page; a **text** moves by the violet grip at its corner (hover, or while typing, because the text itself takes the cursor); **corner handles** resize; while dragging, the item **snaps to the page centre and the 48 px margins with a pink guide line** (`snap.ts`); a picked item carries **Crop** and **Reset** above it. **Double-tap a photo (or Crop) to crop**: the whole photo shows faded around the frame, dragging moves the picture inside it and follows the hand, the wheel or the Zoom slider zooms (`photo-math.ts`: `placePhoto`, `panPhoto`), Done or a tap outside ends it. Where an item went is kept per item in `page.data.layout` (`{dx, dy, s}`, page pixels, keyed like `styles`), so the client's link and the PDF show it too, Undo takes a move back and Reset puts it in its layout place. No migration: it lives in the design's document. **Step 5 (owner, 9 Nov: "check the entire quotation-making system by yourself"):** the rail has **Text** (Add a heading · subheading · a little bit of text) and **Elements** (Photo frame · Line · Box · Circle); each lands on the page in view, picked, and is kept in `page.data.elements` (`designElement`, at most 40 a page, keyed `el.<id>` in `layout` and `styles`, `features/designer/elements.ts`); a picked one carries Colour (shapes), Copy and Delete, and a new empty photo frame is picked so a tap in Uploads fills it. Keys: arrows nudge the picked item (Shift = 10 px), Delete takes an added item off, Ctrl+D copies it, Escape lets go. While the editor is open, toasts (a follow-up reminder too) sit bottom right, never over the foot bar. **Step 6 (owner, 10 Nov: "the designer doesn't come out full ... users get confused"):** every ready-made template is **complete** -- every page written and every frame filled -- and the first and default is **Complete Wedding Package**, the owner's own quotation page for page (cover, About us, Sagan · Mehendi and Cocktail · Bhaat and Rituals · Wedding Ceremony, the ten Wedding Deliverables with "Total Investment: INR 2,10,000/-", Thank You), then Royal Rajasthani, Destination · 3 Days, Intimate, Pre-Wedding + Wedding Combo and the six older ones filled out (`starters.ts`). Their photos are **samples**: The Mulberry Weddings' own (`public/designer/samples`, `designPhoto.sample`), shown only so the studio sees the look -- each carries an amber **Sample · replace** that says so and offers **From the library** or **Upload your own**, and **Send to client and Download PDF are refused while one remains** ("3 sample photos to replace" · Show me, `samplePhotos`); a sample never carries into a new design (`keepStudioPages`, `fillDesign`) or becomes a card's picture. **Photos** in the rail has **Your photos** (uploads) and **Library** -- edited photos from the front of the owner's Drive folder "DIAMOND MEMBERS PORTFOLIO PHOTOS", free for every studio, shipped in `public/designer/library` and listed in `features/designer/library.ts` (mood chips; never rows in Uploads) -- and an empty frame offers the same two buttons. With a project, `pourProject` swaps in only what the project has (its shoots, its quotation deliverables, its extras, its price) and keeps every template page it lacks; a kind the template has no page for goes in after the nearest related page.

- **AI tools 2 and 3: Reply to a lead and the Follow-up sequence maker** (owner, 9 Nov: "build up a great plan first"; no migration). **Reply with AI** (violet, beside WhatsApp in the lead drawer's contact bar and on My leads; `features/crm-ai/ReplySheet.tsx`) writes one WhatsApp reply from the lead (events, city, QR answers, stage, booked/lost, the date clash) and the last 12 messages and notes (`lib/lead-reply.ts`, `lib/lead-writer.ts`): what it is for (First reply · Share packages · Ask for a call · No reply yet · Follow up on quote · Price feels high · Is my date free? · Thank you, booked · Win them back · Something else, the first picked from where the lead is, `defaultIntent`), English · हिन्दी · Hinglish (suggested from the client's own words, `looksLike`) and a tone; the person edits it, then **Open WhatsApp** (wa.me with the text), **Send from your number** (CRM only, while `crm_whatsapp_can_send` allows) or **Copy** -- each kept on the timeline as an outgoing WhatsApp "Reply written with AI". **Prices only from the studio's saved packages** (`invoice_item_presets`), never a discount, date or availability it was not given; a client's words are quoted conversation, never instructions. Routes: `POST /crm/leads/:id/reply(/sent|/send)` (`modules/crm/routes-ai.ts`) and `POST /me/leads/:id/reply(/sent)` (the caller's own lead). **The sequence maker** is `/ai/sequences` (`routes/ai-sequences.tsx`, and **Write with AI** on Settings' sequences): what happened (New enquiry no reply · Quotation sent gone quiet · After the first call · Price felt high · After the shoot · Win back · Something else), how long, how often, which messages (WhatsApp, + call reminders, + email), language and tone; `POST /crm/sequences/write` (`lib/sequence-writer.ts`, one step a line, parsed forgivingly, known `{{words}}` only, hours 6–21, days in order, validated by `sequenceInput`) gives a draft shown for a sample couple, and **Use this sequence** opens the usual `SequenceEditor` (`draft` prop) -- nothing is saved or started until the studio saves it. Every AI writer goes through `lib/ai-write.ts` (`writeWithAi`: key → the studio's daily allowance shared with the assistant → token budget → model → log of the kind only, never a lead's name). A held automatic sequence email now waits in Send now instead of failing.

- **Referral cards: the friend gets an offer, the client gets a reward** (owner, 10 Nov; 0298). Every booked client's project has its referral campaign (0102, one per project), now carrying the **friend's offer** (`friend_reward_type` none · fixed ₹ off · percentage · free_addon, `friend_reward_value/title`, `valid_until`), filled from the studio's **defaults** (`referral_settings`: friend offer, client reward, 3/6/12 months, card look; Settings → Referrals → **Cards & offers**, `ReferralDefaults.tsx`). The project's **Referrals** tab opens on the **Referral card** (`features/referrals/card/`): a 1080×1080 WhatsApp picture drawn on a canvas (`drawReferralCard`, the designer's six looks and fonts, "A GIFT FROM Priya & Rahul", the offer, the code = the campaign slug, a QR to `/refer/<slug>`, valid till), **Share on WhatsApp** (the phone's share sheet with the picture; elsewhere it is saved and wa.me opens with `shareText`) and **Download**; the offer is changed right there. `/refer/<slug>` leads with the friend's offer and asks the friend only for their own details. The friend's enquiry makes a lead linked to it (`crm_leads.referral_submission_id`, `referred_by_name`, a violet **Referred by Priya** chip in the drawer); **when that lead is booked** (converted) the client's reward is **due** with a bell to the owner and the project's creator (`crm_leads_referral_booked`; un-booking puts it back to not due, never a given one), and the friend's **first invoice carries the offer by itself** (`referral_offer_for_project`, `GET /referrals/offer/:project`; flat or % discount, or a ₹0 "(referral gift via Priya)" line) with an amber chip to take it off. Card opens are counted (`referral_card_opened`). **Referral cards is its own page, `/ai/referrals`** (owner audit, 10 Nov: "the card is not even showing up" -- it sat under the project's Wishes tab): **Whose card?** (`ProjectPicker`, the cards already made as chips) opens that client's card; before a pick, a sample card in the studio's defaults shows how it looks (`CardPreview`, `sampleCard`). AI → Referral cards opens it, Referrals' header carries **Make a referral card**, the defaults show the same live preview beside them, and on a project the group is **Referrals & wishes**, opening on **Referral card**. The card itself: the studio's logo or name, "A GIFT FROM" and the client in the look's script, the offer on a band of the accent, then three steps to claim it, the gift code (the slug's last six characters, `cardCode`) and valid-till beside a large QR; the panel says how often it was opened and how many friends enquired and booked, both sides' rewards, the link to copy, and Share on WhatsApp · Download · Copy message.

- **AI tools are two panes, and the hub has no "Coming soon"** (owner audit, 10 Nov: "a lot of empty space is left on the right-hand side"). Every AI writer page uses `features/ai/parts.tsx`: numbered steps on the left (`AiStep`, `AiTile`, `AiChoice`, `aiChip`), the answer on the right (`AiTwoPane`, sticky from 1024 px, stacked and scrolled to below), and before anything is written a violet panel saying what will appear (`AiWaiting`). `/ai` opens on a gradient band and one card per working tool with "Open →"; a tool that lives inside another (Write it for me) is named on that tool's card, never a Coming soon card. **Social media captions** is `/ai/captions` (`routes/ai-captions.tsx`): Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp status; the kind of post; its details typed or filled from a project; language, tone, hashtags and emojis; three captions back to edit and Copy (`POST /designs/captions`, `lib/caption-writer.ts`, on `writeWithAi`), never posted from here and never inventing names, prices or places.

- **Ad scripts: a Meta ad a model reads to camera** (owner, 10 Nov: "the script should be so strong that the ad generates a lot of leads"; no migration). `/ai/ad-scripts` (`routes/ai-ad-scripts.tsx`, a card on `/ai`): the studio's facts in a few taps -- name and city (from its profile), weddings, years, Google rating (each optional; a blank number is never said), what makes it different (chips with + Add, kept on the person's quick list `ad_strength`) and a free line -- then who speaks (female or male model, a couple, the owner), the angle (**Three different ones** by default: how to choose · invite · a wedding moment; or one of how to choose, direct invitation, mistakes, myth vs truth, a wedding moment, dates filling up), हिन्दी (default) · English · Hinglish, 30/45/60 s, tone, the ask (Get Quote button, WhatsApp, call) and an offer. `POST /designs/ad-scripts` (`lib/ad-script-writer.ts` on `writeWithAi`) gives each script a **hook** for 0-3 s, timed parts, on-screen lines, notes for the model and its spoken length from the words (`spokenSeconds`; amber "a little long" past the target); the owner's own Wed Story script is in the prompt as a voice to learn with its name and numbers taken out, never copied. Each card is editable and has **Teleprompter** (`features/ai/Teleprompter.tsx`: big white words on black, scrolling in the script's time, slower/faster, text size, Space and Escape), Copy, Send on WhatsApp and Write again (that one angle). The facts, the choices and the last scripts (with the length they were written for) are kept on the device; nothing is saved on the server or posted. **After the live test (11 Nov):** each language has its own examples and its rule last in both messages (`SCRIPT_LANG`; English came back Hinglish and Hindi hooks in Roman when the only example was Hinglish); every script opens a different way (`hookKindsFor`: truth, call-out, scene, mistake, number; generic "are you planning your wedding?" banned), and Write again sends the other hooks (`avoid_hooks`); numbers are said exactly as given and the speaker never tells a story of their own; the blocks scale with the length (`partTimes`); and an answer in the wrong language (`languageProblem`) is asked for once more, best effort -- a refused retry keeps the first answer.

- **A list of designs shows its actions on the card** (owner, 10 Nov: "no option of deleting ... no editing rights"): Edit (solid) with Rename, Make a copy and Delete as bordered icon buttons, a tick on each card to delete several, and a search; Templates shows the studio's own (Use, Change, Rename, Delete) and the ready-made six (Use this), each opening New design on it. Never only a ⋯ menu.

- **The designer's colours are one picker, with gradients** (owner, 10 Nov: "a lot of colour options ... including gradient"). `features/designer/ColourPicker.tsx` (dark, like the editor): **Solid** -- this design's own colours, Used lately (on the device), ten families of eight (`PALETTE` in `paint.ts`: ivory, gold, blush, maroon, haldi, sage, peacock, navy, plum, charcoal), any colour by picker or hex code; **Gradient** (page backgrounds and shapes) -- eighteen ready-made (`GRADIENTS`: Champagne, Gold dust, Rose gold, Royal maroon, Haldi, Mehendi, Peacock…) or two or three colours of your own in five directions. A paint is stored as a short checked string (`designPaint`/`PAINT_RE` in contracts: `#RRGGBB`, `lg:<angle>:#A:#B[:#C]`, `rg:#A:#B[:#C]`) and drawn by `cssPaint`; nothing else reaches a style. Each page's **Background** button above it, **Every page's background** in Look, a shape's Colour and the text bar's More colours all open it; text stays solid.
