# Frameline photo sharing and Google Drive

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **Frameline is the gallery add-on, joined by sign-in and links, never merged** (client, 12 Oct; the Kamero clone in `E:\BuildOurs\Kamero-Clone`, repo dev-gwv/Frameline, on Cloudflare). A project's **Post-Production → Gallery** (`features/gallery/GalleryTab.tsx`, `routes-gallery.ts`, projects edit) makes one Frameline event per project (**Create gallery**), shows the guests' link, PIN, WhatsApp share and QR, and **Open gallery** signs the person into Frameline at that event. The client's tracking page shows **Your photo gallery** with the PIN (`client_portal_gallery()`, read in a savepoint beside `get_client_portal`). `lib/frameline.ts` signs HS256 tokens with `FRAMELINE_SECRET` (= Frameline's `STUDIOAUTOPILOT_SECRET`), one minute and single-use: `sso` for the browser link (always fetched at the press, the window opened first so it is not blocked), `api` server to server. Frameline matches people by our user id and by email **only** when `email_verified` (team logins may carry any email); the owner (`isOwner`) sets the studio up and everyone else joins as editor. **Billing is Frameline's own** (a separate add-on), except Google Drive galleries, which Pro, Studio Max and the trial cover (below, 0307). A gallery made from here is PIN + registration, face privacy **off** and downloads **all** (`SA_GALLERY` in Frameline's integrations.ts): selfie search is not running in Frameline's production, and its 'own' downloads need a selfie match, so nobody could download. Never promise "find your photos with a selfie" on a screen or in help until it runs. Frameline's side is `apps/api/src/routes/integrations.ts` and `services/partner.ts` there. **Since 0264 the kept row follows Frameline**: every Gallery tab read and every `POST /webhooks/frameline` notice (`modules/webhooks/frameline.ts`, signed by Frameline with the same secret, `iss: frameline`, `purpose: notice`; it only says which project, the server reads the gallery back) saves the PIN, the couple's full-access PIN, photos, visits, guests and expiry through `saveGallerySnapshot` (`lib/gallery-sync.ts`), which rings the bell once per new batch of photos (`photos_announced`). The client's page shows the couple's PIN too, and the card only once the gallery has photos and until it closes (`galleryForClient`, no migration). **A gallery deleted in Frameline** (its own 404, code `not_found`) removes the kept row, so Create gallery shows again; a bare 404, 401/403 or 501 is "isn't linked properly" with Book a support call, never "try again" (`refusalKind`), and an owner whose studio name could not be read is stopped before any call (`nameMissing`), never sent as "Studio". **Projects → Galleries** (`routes/galleries.tsx`, `GET /galleries`) lists every gallery; **Settings → Messaging → Gallery** (`routes/settings/gallery.tsx`, owner only, `GET /galleries/account`) shows the Frameline studio, plan, wallet and **Open Frameline**. Everything else about a gallery (uploads, albums, watermark, selling, Frameline billing) stays in Frameline, reached by Open gallery / Open Frameline -- never rebuilt here. **Google Drive is Frameline's too** (30 Oct; no migration): Frameline's `/studio` answer carries `drive` (offered? connected? as whom? new galleries in Drive by default?) and each gallery its `originalsIn`; `driveOut()` in `lib/frameline.ts` maps it onto `galleryDrive` (contracts). **Import from Google Drive happens on our Gallery tab** (owner, 31 Oct; `features/gallery/DriveImport.tsx`, `modules/projects/routes-gallery-drive.ts`): Google's own picker opens on our page with a token Frameline gives for the studio's Drive (`GET /projects/:id/gallery/drive/picker` → Frameline's partner `/drive/picker`), the studio checks what will come in (each folder an album, renamed in place, what is left out, Standard or Original files) and presses Import; Frameline copies the photos in the background (`/drive/expand`, `/events/:p/drive-imports`, the same code its own import window runs, `services/drive-actions.ts` there), and a progress line with Stop sits on the tab until it ends. The picker needs `docs.google.com` in `frame-src` and our site on the Picker key's referrers in the Google Cloud project. When Drive is not connected the owner's button opens Frameline's Drive settings; anyone else is told to ask the owner. **Import from Google Drive is Pro and above, and every Pro and Studio Max card says "Photo Sharing App via Google Drive"** (0293, owner 5 Nov; Compare plans' row has the same words; a trial keeps it) (owner, 31 Oct: "diamond pro and above ... for the basic plan users it won't be available"; 0292): `plans.includes` carries `drive_import` on every Pro and Studio Max row, regular and Diamond, `company_drive_import()` refuses only a running Starter plan (a trial keeps it), the Drive routes answer 402 and the Starter button carries a lock with Upgrade; Compare plans has the row. Connecting Drive, Create gallery and the gallery itself stay on every plan. **How it works** (`features/gallery/GalleryGuide.tsx`) sits beside the gallery's buttons, on Settings → Gallery and on Projects → Galleries, and the assistant's `gallery-google-drive` and `gallery-frameline` articles say the same. Also a "Keep full-size photos in Google Drive" switch on Create gallery once Drive is connected (`originals_in`; Frameline answers 409 `drive_not_connected` → `drive_off`), and where the full-size photos are. Settings → Gallery has a **Google Drive** card (`POST /galleries/account/open` with `to: 'drive'` opens Frameline's Drive settings). Never rebuild the connect flow or the copy here: the Drive connection, the copy and the photo limits stay Frameline's.

- **Pro and Studio Max cover Google Drive galleries in Frameline, up to a number of photos kept** (owner, 17 Nov; 0307, 0308; **the studio-facing parts below are hidden for now by the bullet above, 0309** -- the cover itself runs; Frameline's migration 0018 and `services/sa-cover.ts` there). Importing from Drive copies each photo into Frameline's storage (R2: the full file and a 2048 px copy) and the gallery is served from there, so it costs storage while it is kept. **Pro 25,000 photos kept, Studio Max 75,000, the trial 3,000, a platform admin's own studio 75,000, Starter none** (`plans.limits.gallery_photos`, `gallery_cover(company)`), plus what the platform owner adds after a WhatsApp sale (**Add gallery photos…** in Studio Access Manager's Actions, `companies.gallery_photos_extra`, `platform_add_gallery_photos`). Every token we sign carries `cover` = {plan, until, photos} (`personOf` → `framelineClaims`; null on Starter; left out when it could not be read, so Frameline keeps the last), and Give plan, Extend, Custom expiry and Add gallery photos push it at once (`pushCover` → `POST /integrations/studioautopilot/cover`). While covered, Frameline counts Drive imports against the cover (deleting a gallery frees room), never its own quota or trial; full is 402 with words that say WhatsApp, and our import toast offers **WhatsApp us**, not Upgrade (`isCoverFull`). Uploads from a computer or phone stay on Frameline's own plan, and **every buy button in Frameline is WhatsApp us** (its Plan page). The Google Drive card on Photo sharing and Settings → Gallery shows "4,200 of 25,000 photos kept · included in your Pro plan until …" with **Ask for more photos** (`CoverLine`, `cover-text.ts`); the help article `gallery-plan-photos`, the `/learn` part **Share the photos** and How it works say the same; every owner got one bell note (`news:gallery_cover`). **Studio Access Manager shows each studio's plan** ("Diamond Pro, monthly" under its status, and in View details; `platform_list_studios` gains `plan_key`, `plan_name`, `gallery_photos_extra` in 0308 -- copy it from there).
