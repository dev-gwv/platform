# Support calls, tickets, WhatsApp support and email

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **The owner gets a Monday email** (built, but held by "No email goes out by itself" below until the owner switches automatic email on; 0258, `lib/weekly-email.ts` on the hourly cron): Monday from 8 am IST, the owner alone, once a week (`weekly_email_mark`), and nothing in a week with nothing to say. Money in last week (`status = 'paid'` only), overdue and due this week; this week's shoot days and how many have nobody booked; late edits and edits due; new leads and booked. Each block is a sentence with one button. The owner switches it off on My profile (beside the morning email) or from "Stop the Monday email" in its footer (`/stop-emails?w=`).

- **No email goes out by itself** (owner, 26 Oct: "turn off all the automatic sending of emails ... only when we trigger it at some place manually"; before that, 12 and 14 Oct: owner only, one a day). `deliver()` in `lib/email.ts` sends only `MANUAL_MAIL_KINDS` -- what a person pressed for: their own email confirmation and password reset, a team invite, a document, receipt or terms someone pressed **Send** on (`client_doc`), crew terms sent with the email box ticked (`team_terms`), and the platform's test send -- to whoever it is for. Everything the app would send on its own (the morning and Monday emails, "your plan is ending", alert copies, setup nudges, "client agreed", sequences, queued messages) is held and logged as skipped, and the cron does not even work them out (`automaticEmailOff`). `EMAIL_EVERY_KIND=on` on the server lifts it. Never route around the gate with a direct call to Resend; a new automatic email stays off until the owner asks for it.

- **Support call alerts are ours, careful, and the platform admins' alone** (owner, 24 and 26 Oct; 0280, 0283, `docs/support-calls.md`). **Bookings come from each person's main Google Calendar, only the entries their appointment schedule made** (owner, 30 Oct: "Booked by" in the description, `isBooking`; the rest of their day is never posted) (two since 0290, read one by one; slot 2's refs are `gcal2:`, so one calendar never cancels the other's calls) (owner, 26 Oct: no booking-app notice, nothing over-built): its private iCal address is pasted on Platform → Support calls, and the five-minute messages tick reads it (`lib/support-calendar.ts` parses and diffs, `lib/support-calendar-sync.ts` saves) -- a new entry is booked, a changed time moved, a removed or cancelled entry cancelled; the first read after an address is set only notes what is there and posts nothing; an empty answer never cancels anything; the studio is found by the booker's email. The booking page is a Google Calendar appointment schedule that writes into that calendar; the booking app is not used (owner, 27 Oct). The address is a secret: kept on `platform_settings`, shown masked, never audited. Studio AutoPilot sends every WhatsApp, from fixed templates filled from the booking -- **never AI-written**. To the person who booked only when they ticked WhatsApp, at most 6 a booking (`BOOKER_CAP`; booked/moved, a reminder an hour before, cancelled, and since 0312 the missed-call link and its repeat; Meta utility templates from the official number). To the team: one post per event in the support group by the **group bot** (`deploy/wa-bot`, Baileys on Node in its own container, a **spare SIM, never the business number**, only groups it is in, ≥5 s apart with a pause and "typing…", 20/h and 100/day on the page and 30/150 in the bot itself, never reads or answers; **since 0310 (owner, 18 Nov) it may also write to whoever booked, two fixed messages at most and never a link** -- a confirmation, and a "reply here if we couldn't connect" ten minutes into the call while the ticket has no note -- so the chat is ready on that WhatsApp; behind **Message the person who booked from this number**, the number read from the booking page's description by `phoneOf`, `POST /dm` with 6/h, 20/day and 3 per number a week in the bot), and at 9 am the day's calls; when the bot is off or cannot post, each team number gets the official `support_call_team` template. Everything waits in `support_call_outbox` (one row per `dedupe_key`, drained at once and on the five-minute messages tick, `lib/support-call-store.ts`; rules in `lib/support-calls.ts`). **Platform → Support calls** (`routes/platform/support-calls.tsx`, `requirePlatformAdmin`) shows the bot's QR and state, the group, the switches, numbers, caps, template names, bookings and every message; everything starts off. The bot is reached only on the compose network with `WA_BOT_SECRET` and is started by `deploy.sh`. The developer's Mac tool (`whatsapp-cli`, Accessibility on WhatsApp Desktop, driven by an AI) cannot run on the VPS and is not used.

- **Every support call is a ticket, and the assistant keeps it** (owner, 11 Nov: "I am the only one ... make it something that will ease my work"; 0300, `docs/support-calls.md`). A ticket opens with each booking (`support_tickets`, service-only; `followBooking` in `lib/support-ticket-store.ts`), closes with a cancel and is asked about again when moved. **75 minutes before** the call the assistant writes a three-line brief from the studio's own data (plan and days left, what it made, days in the app, where it got stuck, earlier tickets, what they wrote; `studioFacts`, `factsText`) and posts it in the support group with the ticket's link; **10 minutes after** the call ends it posts "How did the call go?" with the link. On the ticket page (`/platform/support-calls/tickets/$id`, `routes/platform/support-ticket.tsx`) the owner records **one voice note** (Hindi or English; Groq Whisper on the assistant's own key, `transcribe`) or types it, and the AI fills the summary, the type (Showing how · Bug · Plan & billing · Feature request · Other), where it stands (Open · Waiting on you · Waiting on them · Solved), the next step and its date -- all changeable with a tap. The 9 am post adds "N tickets open, M waiting on you" with each late step. AI text goes **only** to the owner's support group, never to a studio; when the AI is down the plain facts go instead, and a note it cannot read stays as the summary. It spends the platform's token budget, never a studio's daily allowance. Tickets come from booked calls and, since 0304, from WhatsApp. The **Tickets** card heads Platform → Support calls. **Step 2 (0301):** **Write the follow-up for me** drafts the WhatsApp to the studio in the call's language with up to two tutorial links (`/help?play=<key>` opens the video at once; the AI picks keys, we add the links) -- **Send on WhatsApp** (the owner's own WhatsApp, to the booking's number, else the owner's or `invoice_phone`), **Copy**, and once the official API is on **Send from our number** (`support_call_followup` template, `support_wa_tpl_followup`); a Bug ticket has **Create GitHub issue** (dev-gwv/platform, prefilled, never a phone or email); a note that says the studio is to do something the app can see (`watch`: project, invoice, team, lead, shoot, client, gallery, plan) closes the ticket by itself once its data shows it after the call started, with a line in the group (`autoCloseTickets`, `lib/support-ticket-followups.ts`); and Monday from 9 am one post sums up the week and names the screen to fix first (`queueWeeklySummary`).

- **Studios can write to support on WhatsApp, and the assistant answers first** (owner, 12 Nov: "the problems that it can solve on its own, it can handle those ... the ones which need me, it notifies me"; 0304, lifting 0273 for this one door). A message to the official number (`WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID`, routed in `POST /webhooks/whatsapp`) is a **chat ticket** (`support_tickets.source = 'chat'`, its messages in `support_ticket_messages`, one open ticket per number, the studio found by a login's or the invoice phone's last ten digits). The assistant (`lib/support-chat-store.ts`, rules in `lib/support-chat.ts`) answers how-to and where-is-it from the same help as the in-app assistant (`ask()` + `WHATSAPP_RULES`: plain text, six lines, the studio's language) and adds "Did that fix it?"; a short thanks closes the ticket, two days of silence closes it, and **it hands to the owner** -- status Waiting on you, one fixed "passed to our support team" line to the studio, a post in the support group with the ticket's link -- for a person asked for, money, a plan change, lost data, something broken, anger, two answers that did not help, no answer in the help, 15 answers in a day, or the switch off. **It never changes data, a plan or money and never promises a date or fix.** The owner replies on the ticket page (`ChatCard`, **Send on WhatsApp**: free text inside 24 hours of their last message, the `support_call_followup` template after). A booked call from the same studio or number joins its open chat ticket. Off until **Let the assistant answer studios on WhatsApp** (`support_wa_chat_on`) on Platform → Support calls; while off, messages are still kept and every one waits for the owner. It spends the platform's token budget, logged as `[support:chat]`.

- **A missed support call gets the booking page, when the owner says so** (owner, 18 Nov: "send new meeting links to the user whose meetings were missed, so they can rejoin"; 0312). The owner taps **Missed** on the ticket (the after-call group post says "Didn't happen? Open it and tap Missed."); nothing guesses from Google Meet. If **Send a new booking link when a call is missed** is on (Platform → Support calls, off by default) and they ticked WhatsApp, our official number sends the fixed utility template `support_call_missed` (`support_wa_tpl_missed`: name, the call's time, that calendar's booking page) and once more 24 hours later, unless the ticket is solved or taken back, or they booked again from the same email or number (`planMissedMessages` in `lib/support-calls.ts`, `markMissed` / `missedStillDue` in `lib/support-call-store.ts`). Both count in `BOOKER_CAP`, now 6. When our number cannot send, the card says why and offers **Send on WhatsApp** from the owner's own phone with the words written (`missedByHandText`). **Not missed** takes it back and stops what has not gone. The ticket waits on them (`missed_at`). Never AI-written.
