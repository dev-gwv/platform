# Sign-up, setup, tutorials, help and the assistant

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **The help bubble is teal with a white ring, never the page's purple, and never sits on a sticky Save / Next bar** (owner, 5 Nov: it merged into Next). A sticky action bar carries `data-sticky-actions`, and `styles.css` keeps a lane free on its right for the bubble on a wider screen; a page without one ends in 4.5rem of room so its last button scrolls clear of the bubble. The wizard's Next bar sits at the foot of the screen on a short step. **Toasts are top centre, under the top bar** (audit, 5 Nov: top right covered the bell, the account menu and a drawer's close).

- **A new account starts at step 1 of setup: Add your team.** Sign-up never follows a left-over `?redirect=` (signing out on Leads leaves `?redirect=/leads` on the sign-in page; a new studio followed it and skipped setup). The first page says **Add your team** in bold with one button, **Add your team**, that opens the bulk list straight away (no one-by-one choice in setup; the Team page keeps both); email confirmation never gates any of it; the three steps show once, in the setup bar, not again in the welcome card. Opening or refreshing the app while setup is unfinished takes an owner back to their current step (`useResumeSetup`, once per load; moving around after that is free).

- **Book a support call is the way to a person** (owner, 19 Oct; named and drawn this way on 21 Oct: "support call", the headphones, and it should stand out). It is always the solid teal button with the headphones (`SUPPORT_CALL_CLASS`, `Headphones`), never an outline or a calendar icon, and it says **Book a support call** ("Book a 15-minute support call" on Plan & billing and Getting started). One link, `platform_settings.support_call_url` (0272: the owner's booking page; set on the Help console, else `ONBOARDING_CALL_URL`), sent with the public Help settings (`GET /public/help` → `support_call_url`) so **everyone signed in** sees it (owner, 19 Oct: "accessible anywhere and anytime"). **Support call sits in the top bar on every page** (`HeaderBookCall`, solid teal pill, the label from 1360px, the icon alone below; not on a phone, where the corner help button carries it). `BookCallButton` / `useCallUrl` (`features/help/BookCall.tsx`) also draws it in the help bubble (assistant on or off), the top bar's assistant panel, sidebar Help, Plan & billing ("Rather talk it through first?"), Getting started ("Want us to set it up with you?"), the "Stuck? Watch how" toast and the "Couldn't show" box; the assistant's hand-over answers carry it too. **The link is a Google Calendar appointment schedule** (owner, 27 Oct: the booking app was removed; 29 Oct: no Calendly anywhere), and it always opens in a new tab -- never framed. **Two people take calls** (owner, 29 Oct; 0290, `support_calendars` slots 1 and 2: name, booking page, iCal address, all set on Platform → Support calls → Google Calendars, not the Help console): with two booking pages set, every Book a support call opens a small chooser, "Pick a time on Google Calendar with:" and one teal button per person (`CallChooser`, `useCallLinks`, `openSupportCall` for a toast); with one it opens directly. Single-link uses (the assistant's `call_url`, emails) take the first person's. Written support requests (Tell the team, Your requests, the support Google Sheet, 0271) were removed in 0273; the one written way in since is WhatsApp to the official number (0304, owner 12 Nov, below) -- never an in-app form or inbox.

- **Help is one message away.** A round **help bubble** sits in the bottom-right corner of every signed-in page (`features/help/HelpBubble.tsx`, z-30, above the phone's bottom bar): the assistant's chat once it is switched on and the server has `AI_API_KEY`, and always WhatsApp us · Email us (`SupportButtons`) and Book a call. Help also sits at the foot of the sidebar (`features/help/HelpPanel.tsx`): "We're one message away" with **WhatsApp us** (green) and **Email us**, each opened with the studio, person, role, plan, page and time already written (`supportMessage`) -- the person still presses Send; a button shows only once its contact is set on `/platform/help`. Then "Watch how this page works" (when the page has a tutorial), **All tutorials** (`/help`, public) and the questions studios ask (`help_faqs`, 0236). Every `PageHeader` carries **Watch how · 40 s** only on a page a tutorial is about (`TUTORIALS[].pages` in `features/help/tutorials.ts`). A tutorial is a real recording of the app, under a minute, 1080p MP4 with a soft music bed and a `.jpg` poster in `apps/web/public/help/`; a new one adds its key to `TUTORIALS` and `SHIPPED`. A link set on `/platform/help` replaces a shipped video or adds one for another page.

- **Help shows where people get stuck, then steps back.** Each job a new studio learns carries its how-to video where the job is done -- `useLearn(key)` / `LearnCard` (`features/help/LearnCard.tsx`): Add your team, one person, bulk add, New client and each step of Create project, one video each. If someone seems stuck (45 s with nothing moving, Save/Next refused twice, the dialog closed twice unsaved -- `stuck-rules.ts`), the video offers itself in the corner once. A card retires per person once the studio has done that job twice, the video was watched to 90 %, or "Don't show again"; all get-started cards go after 3 projects or 14 days (`learning.ts`, `users.hints.learn`, `GET /auth/learn`). The small "Watch how" beside a page title stays for good. A new video joins `TUTORIALS`/`SHIPPED` and, for a card, `LEARN_AREA`.

- **Every tutorial is in English and Hindi, and the whole app is one guide away.** `/learn` ("How to use Studio AutoPilot", public, `routes/learn.tsx`, chapters in `features/help/guide.ts`) walks every job in order, each with its video and its steps written out in both languages (button names stay in English, in bold): **For the studio** (Start here · Win the booking · Shoot and edit · Money · Your team's time) and **For your team** (staff land on it). "I have done this" ticks are kept on the device. It is the first line in Help, a one-time card on everyone's Home (`GuideCard`, `users.hints.guide`: gone once opened, closed or seen on 8 visits), and a banner on `/help`. Every video has a Hindi twin in `apps/web/public/help/hi/` -- the same steps with Hindi captions (`TUT_LANG=hi` in the recorder, captions from `hi.mjs`). **Every video is spoken by one female voice** (Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, Chirp 3 HD *Aoede*, `en-IN` and `hi-IN`; the owner's Google project has the API on) reading the approved script (`vo.mjs` in the recorder: a title line, one line per step, an end line); each step waits for her to finish, and the music drops under her; English · हिन्दी in the player is remembered per device (`?lang=hi` opens Hindi). A new tutorial ships both, joins `SHIPPED_HI` and carries `hi: { title, blurb }`; a new job joins a chapter in `guide.ts` in both languages (the test fails otherwise).

- **A video always matches the screen it shows.** When a change touches a screen a tutorial records (a button renamed, a step moved or added, a page redesigned, a module moved), re-record that tutorial in the same pull request, English and Hindi, with the recorder in `tools/tutorials/` (`t-<key>.mjs`, voice from `vo.mjs`, Hindi captions from `hi.mjs`; how-to in its README), and update its `seconds`. Which screens each video shows is in `t-<key>.mjs`. A pull request that changes such a screen without a new recording says why in its description.

- **Platform → Usage → Where people stop** shows, for each screen a new studio learns (Add your team, New client, each step of Create project -- the `useLearn` keys), how many visits opened it, finished it, were refused or left, with the worst one or two as a sentence of what to fix (`features/platform/stuck.ts`, `GET /platform/usage/stuck`). `useLearn` records it through `trackFlow` (`usage_events`, module `flow`) whether or not its card still shows; nothing typed on a screen is ever sent. A new screen a studio must learn gets a `useLearn` key and joins `FLOWS`.

- **The assistant answers in English, हिन्दी or Hinglish** (client, 12 Oct). The first time someone opens it, the panel asks which (`LanguagePicker` in `features/assistant/AssistantPanel.tsx`, in both scripts) and keeps the answer on their row (`users.hints.assistant_lang`, so it is asked once per person, not per device); three chips under the question box change it, and picking Hindi or Hinglish also plays the tutorials in Hindi. Every question carries `language` (`askRequest`, default `en`), and `lib/assistant.ts` puts one `LANGUAGE_LINE` **last** in the system message -- never in the stable block, or each language would be its own cached prefix. Hinglish is Hindi in English letters, never Devanagari; button and menu names stay English in all three. Every sentence the server writes itself (escalations, failures, "busy", "asked enough today") has all three versions (`SAY` in `lib/assistant.ts`, `ai-budget.ts`, `ASKED_ENOUGH` in the router), and so does the panel's chrome (`features/assistant/copy.ts`). Retrieval reads Hindi and Hinglish through `lib/hindi-words.ts` (Devanagari and Roman words → the English the help uses; nukta and chandrabindu folded): a Hindi question that matched nothing was answered from memory with invented buttons, so **a starter question or a common Hindi phrasing that stops matching is a bug** -- `retrieve.test.ts` checks them. A new word goes under the English word the help uses for it.

- **The sign-in and sign-up page credits its maker** (owner, 24 Oct): under the forms, `DesignerCredit` (`shared/ui/designer-credit.tsx`) reads "Designed & developed by Abdullah Ansari & Team" with "(India's only Photography Business Coach)" beneath, in the wordmark's navy and gold. Keep it on `/login` in every mode.

- **New studios get the setup videos before they meet the app** (owner, 10 Nov: "they feel a little insecure when they have to log in"). `/start` (the same page as `/help/setup`, public, `routes/help-setup.tsx`) is **Set up Studio AutoPilot, step by step**: numbered steps from `features/onboarding/setup-videos.ts` -- 1 Create your account and sign in (`signup`), 2 Add your team (`team-bulk`), 3 Add your first client, 4 Create your first project (with its four part videos), 5 Send the quotation, 6 Make it yours (optional) -- each its video in English or हिन्दी (`useHelpLang`, `?lang=hi`), every click written out in both languages, **I have done this** (on the device) and one button into the app. The emails' `#team`, `#client`, `#project` anchors never change. It is linked as a teal pill under every mode of the sign-in page and on the home page (`SetupVideosLink`), first in Help, and from the setup bar ("Watch the setup video"). A change to any screen these videos show re-records them (the 5 Nov "who will shoot it?" question broke `project` and `project-deliverables` until 10 Nov); a new setup step joins `SETUP_VIDEOS` and its test.

- **A new person is walked through the videos, never sent a demo** (owner, 12 Nov: "they do not need to have a demo video ... the system should be able to take them"). Three parts, no migration: (1) **Set up Studio AutoPilot, step by step** (`features/onboarding/SetupVideosCard.tsx`, rules in `setup-progress.ts`) is the first card on an owner's or admin's Home -- during setup it stands in for the setup card, after it it comes before Getting started -- with the six `/start` steps, each ticked by the studio's own data (team, client, project, quotation, logo; never a box to tick), "2 of 6 done" and a bar, and the next step amber with **Watch** (the video plays on the card, `TutorialPlayer`) and **Do this now** (`?from=setup` while setup is open). It goes once the five required steps are done, after 60 days, or on its close (`users.hints.setup_videos`); the optional sixth never holds it up. (2) **Learn your side of Studio AutoPilot** (`TeamVideosCard.tsx`, `team-videos.ts`) is a new team member's first card on Home: their day, handing in an edit, attendance (only while it is on) and leave, each played on the card and ticked once watched (`users.hints.team_videos`), for a login's first 60 days; then the guide card. (3) **A welcome in the bell, once per person** (`POST /auth/welcome`, `lib/welcome.ts`, called by `useWelcomeOnce` in `AppShell`): "Welcome! Set up Studio AutoPilot in 6 short videos" → `/start` for whoever sets up a studio under 30 days old, "Welcome to <studio>! Learn your side in a few short videos" → `/learn` for a login under 30 days old; the server writes `users.hints.welcome` either way, so older logins are never sent one. The setup bar's **Watch the setup video** plays on the page, never sends the person away mid-step. The recorder puts these cards away for whoever a tutorial signs in as (`quietCards` in `tools/tutorials/lib.mjs`). When someone asks for a demo, the answer is `studioautopilot.in/start`. **(4) A pop-up that keeps coming back until they start** (owner, 14 Nov: "there should be multiple pop-ups until they start to use the system"): **Set up your Studio AutoPilot** (`features/onboarding/SetupPopup.tsx`, lazy in `AppShell`, owner and admins only) plays the next step's video in English or हिन्दी beside the six steps ticked from the data, with **Do this now**, **All steps from zero** (`/start`), **Later** (4 hours) and **Remind me tomorrow**. `popupDue` in `setup-progress.ts`: every visit while the studio has no project; once it has one, again after 3 days with nothing made (`last_work_at` on `GET /settings/getting-started`) while the studio is under 60 days old; never once the five required steps are done. Once a visit, 2.5 s after the page settles, never over another dialog, a setup step page (`?from=setup`), `/projects/new`, `/start`, Help, the guide, Platform or the designer.

- **A new studio's first screens stay uncluttered** (owner, 20 Nov: "the system is cluttered"). **Sign-up is wide** (`routes/login.tsx`: two columns, `max-w-[46rem]`; sign-in stays narrow). **Leads has no "Get leads running" checklist** -- an empty studio sees the empty pipeline (the checklist and its files are gone). **Home's setup card shows only the next step** (Watch · Do this now) with **See all 6 steps** to open the rest; no support-call button on it (the top bar has one). **Setup videos is in the top bar** for the owner and admins in a studio's first 60 days (`HeaderSetupVideos` in `features/onboarding/SetupVideosLink.tsx`, `showSetupPill`; violet pill, label from 1360px, icon below, not on a phone): it opens `studioautopilot.in/start`. **The Team page has one heading**: search · **Filter** (type, status, role and sort only once pressed) · **More** (Invite by link, Roles & access, Export CSV) · **Add Team Member**; no Freelance tab (it was the Type filter twice), and Pending invitations shows only while there is one. Setup's Add your team step has no welcome card above it: the setup bar already says the three steps.
