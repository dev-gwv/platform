# Projects, quotation, shoots, crew booking, post-production and the client link

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **Every project page carries the journey line** (`features/projects/ProjectJourney.tsx`): Quotation · Invoice · Team · Deliver, the next step named with one button. The quotation page shows only the document, a one-row toolbar (Back · Client can see it · Send to client ▾ · Print · ⋯ for terms, refresh and display options) and the journey's next step (the owner: "only the quotation should be visible"). The studio's view is compact on screen (`compact` → `.quotation-compact`, `@media screen` only); print and the client's link are the full paper. Creating a project always lands on the quotation -- a new studio's first project too (setup closes on the way, no "studio is set up" dialog). The quotation page carries the journey bar: "Happy with the quotation? Create the invoice" (with Send to client beside it); that opens the **whole project** (client, project and the package line filled in) with the advance already received ticked under "Already received for this project" and "₹X to collect" said in a sentence (`attach_payment_ids` on `POST /billing/invoices` links those payments to the invoice, so nothing is counted twice), and saving it ("Save the invoice" when only the advance is on it, "Save and record payment" when a new payment is; never "Save as draft" with money on it) goes straight to the Shoots tab with the first day short of people in view and its Assign team pulsing. An invoice counts the quotation done, link or no link. A new step in the studio's flow joins that line, not a guide box.

- **The project page has eight tabs** (`features/projects/ProjectTabs.tsx`): Overview · Quotation · Shoots · Post-Production (Work · Work to review · Task Management · Gallery) · Terms · Finance (Billing · Expenses · Cost sheet · Payouts) · Data · Referrals & wishes (Referral card · Wishes). A new project view joins a group (`PROJECT_GROUPS`) and its sub-tab row, never the strip. The sub-tab row is bordered chips with icons (`SectionTabs variant="chips"`), the open one tinted in the studio's colour, with an amber count of what waits ("Work to review 2") -- never grey underlined text (the owner could not see them). View ids (`billing`, `expenses`, `costs`, `deliverables`, `completed_work`, `tasks`…) never change: links, `tracking.ts` and notifications stored in the database use them. (The owner: twelve tabs were "a lot of chaos".)

- **The Overview is money and work only**: no share-with-client or referral card. The client link is under More → Share with client; asking for a referral is the Referrals tab.

- **The Shoots tab starts with the days.** One row above them: **Bulk assign team** first, solid and full size, then + Add event (owner, 5 Nov: "Bulk should be clearly visible"), no data strip, no "See all shoots". The event chips fold behind "+ Add event" once a day exists; the "Who this day needs" chips show only while a day has no roles; no "Next: …" line on a card. Green that reads: a filled role and a person whose data is in are `bg-success/15` (`dataRowTone` in `features/data/stage.ts`), a fully staffed day gets `bg-success/[0.06]` with its green top bar. The card's Assign team button is solid and full size, never outline or small.

- **The data dialog is short and remembers.** Copied by · Received on, then one line per copy (disk + status; folder/link on "+ Folder or link"); type, size, cards, label and notes under "More details". A new record starts from the studio's last copier and main/backup disks (`data-defaults:<company>` in the browser, ids only).

- **A function has a Start time and a Duration (अवधि).** `features/shoots/ShootWhenFields.tsx` (Date · Start time · Duration) is the one way a shoot's hours are set -- in the wizard, the edit dialogs and Assign team alike; Duration is `shared/ui/duration-field.tsx` (1–12 h chips + Custom), required in the wizard, and `end_at = start + hours` (no duration column). A card shows `4:00 pm–9:00 pm · 5 h`, or one amber pill "Set start time & duration" until it is known. (The owner: the team's hours are planned from it.) A function also carries **Guests** (one number, `shoots.guests` / `crm_lead_functions.guests`, 0251), shown as "~400 guests" on the card, in Assign team's subtitle and as a column on the quotation; a lead's guests ride into the shoot on booking. A function added from a chip has no date until one is set ("Set the date", amber) -- never today's date as a stand-in.

- **Every function is asked for its team before the wizard goes on** (owner, 5 Nov). `stepErrors` refuses Event days while a function has no date, start time or duration (audit, 5 Nov: "Set the date for Haldi."), and a refused Next opens and scrolls to the first day with a gap. **A function with no requirements is a question, not a wall** (owner, 5 Nov, later the same day): Next asks "Haldi has no shoot requirements yet" with **Add shoot requirements now** (amber) or **Add them later** and a "Remember my choice for next time" tick (`RequirementsChoiceDialog`, `onlyRequirementsMissing`, `stepErrors(draft, { requirementsLater })`); the answer is kept per person (`users.hints.requirements_choice`, `{choice: 'now'|'later'}`), Event days then shows one line saying what was chosen with **Ask me each time** to undo it, and saving or undoing says so in a toast, and an empty card's **Add requirements** is full size, amber and nudging (`RequirementsDialog`). **The price step offers the studio's packages**: its saved items with a price are one-tap chips under Package cost, and a price typed by hand gets "Save ₹1,50,000 as a package for next time?" (`SavedPackages` in `StepBilling.tsx`, the invoice's saved items, `/billing/items`), so a price is typed once. The aim is fewer keystrokes, more taps.

- **The Billing step takes one advance.** "Advance received (optional)", no "+ Add advance payment" button; more money is recorded on the project afterwards.

- **Assign team shows each person's day in plain words; there is no gap setting.** No strip of date, Change and Gap at the top (the owner found it confusing): the subtitle reads `Engagement · Fri, 23 Oct · 3–6 PM`, and only a shoot with no time asks for one ("No time set for this shoot yet." → **Set the time**). How long it takes to get from one booking to the next is the planner's judgement. Every name says, from `features/shoots/assign.ts`: "Free all day" / "Free at this time · also booked 7–9 PM · Sangeet (Mehta Wedding)" / "On leave that day" / "Busy 3–6 PM · Engagement (Sharma Wedding)" / "Already on this shoot as …"; booked people carry "Also booked …". Hovering (or tapping) that line anywhere a person is booked -- Assign team, the project's Shoots tab, Team Booking, the people grid -- opens their whole day (`features/shoots/PersonDay.tsx`: each booking, then "Free before 3 PM, 6–7 PM, after 9 PM"). Red only for a true overlap. The per-person duration chip and pencil stay.

- **The studio logo and a person's photo are uploads, under 1 MB** (`shared/ui/photo-upload.tsx`, "Keep it under 1 MB."), saved the moment they land -- never a URL box. The API serves `/public/files/:id` with `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin` because the app and the API are two origins; the baseline `same-origin` header hid every logo in an `<img>`.

- **After Book, Done is the next thing.** In Assign team, once someone is booked and nothing is left to book, Done turns solid with the `ipc-nudge` and Book goes quiet. Bulk assign the same: after a booking it says "Asha is booked on 3 shoots. Pick someone else, or press Done." (never "cannot take any of these"), and with two or more days ticked one **Payout for each ticked day** box fills them all.

- **Start editing asks who.** Start editing on a deliverable with nobody on it opens "Who will edit Wedding Teaser?" (`features/projects/GiveWorkDialog.tsx`): editors first, each with how much they already have ("2 in hand · 1 late", `GET /projects/deliverables/workload`), the due date filled from the studio's work days, a line of brief, then **Start editing with Nitin**. The editor chip, the bulk bar (tick cards on the Work tab) and the drawer open the same dialog; the editor and due-date chips are amber and dashed while missing. Whoever gave the work (`deliverables.assigned_by`, 0229) is told when it is handed in.

- **A project's money says what share it is.** Under Received "20% collected", under Still to collect "80% to come · ₹20,000 promised", and with profit access a fourth box, Margin ("94% · after crew & expenses"), from the same booked profit as the Finance tab's card and Billing > Profit & Loss, so the page never shows two margins.

- **A shoot day's line carries its crew cost and its data in one sentence.** "Crew ₹12,000 · not final" for whoever sees money, and once the day has passed "Data: 1 of 2 handed in · 1 backed up" (amber, opens Record everyone's cards while any card is out); a role's chip reads "1/2 backed up". The data dialog shows disk names by example ("e.g. WD-001", "e.g. SEA-002").

- **What the quotation shows from the project has an Edit that goes there.** Deliverables and Event schedule carry a screen-only Edit to the project's Work and Shoots tabs; never on print or the client's link.

- **A client's Accept rings the bell** for the owner and the project's creator (0231); it never books, invoices or counts as money. **Every studio starts with "Sample — Wedding"** in Project templates (0232), marked Sample until edited.

- **A person has usual rates; a day's crew can be copied.** Beside the day rate (`freelancer_rate`) a person carries a wedding-day and a half-day rate (0233, Edit → pay; pay keys, so only salary handlers set or see them). Assign team fills a payout with `suggestedRate` -- 5 h or less takes the half-day rate, a wedding function (Wedding, Pheras, Shaadi…) the wedding rate, anything else the day rate -- and says "Pay ₹12,000 (their wedding rate)". Once a day has its crew, **Book the same people for Wedding and Reception?** (`CopyTeamBar`, `sameCrewPlan`) books them on the project's coming days in the same roles at each day's hours; busy, on-leave, full or unneeded seats are skipped and the toast names who and why.

- **See it as the client; plan parts carry dates.** Send to client ▾ starts with **See it as the client** (`?as=client` on the quotation page): the full paper the client's link shows, with "This is what your client sees. Opening it here isn't counted as a client view." -- nothing is issued, opened or counted. On the Billing tab each plan part reads its date, worked out from the shoots (`planDueDate`: "Before the wedding" = 7 days before the shoot named Wedding, "On the haldi" = that day, "On booking" = the day agreed; "On delivery" stays undated), and Add payment from client on a part says "₹X is left. It stays due on 5 Nov." The client's own copy is never changed by it.

- **Team Booking has a Calendar.** Shoots · **Calendar** · People · Conflicts: the month Monday-first (`CalendarView`, `calendarWeeks`, `dayFill`); a day stays plain until every shoot on it has its team, then turns green ("Team set"), else a quiet "2 to fill"; tapping a shoot opens Assign team. It follows the same search and month as the Shoots view.

- **A studio picks how work is delivered** (Settings → Delivery stages, owner only; `companies.delivery_flow`, 0234). **Check before it goes** (Full, every studio until now): a hand-in waits in Review. **Handed in is delivered** (Simple): a hand-in is approved and Delivered as it lands, the board reads To do · Editing · Delivered, every Move-to list drops Review (`stepsFor(flow)`, `lanesFor(stages, flow, inReview)`), and the hand-in dialog says "Hand in". The database decides, never the app: a Full studio's hand-in is always `review_required`; a manager can still send a Simple hand-in back (Editing reopens) and its editor can fix the link. Switching never removes a stage; work already in Review keeps one plain Review lane until it moves on. On a Full studio's board, a drag that would take unchecked work (To do, Editing, Changes requested) past the first review stop asks **Does this need a review?** -- "Yes, send to With manager" / "No, move to Approved" (`skipsReview`, `reviewLane` in `features/board/board-model.ts`); a team member's drag goes to review without asking.

- **A project from a template gets its due dates.** Applying a template dates each new deliverable the way the wizard would (`fillProjectDueDates` in `services/api/src/lib/due-fill.ts`, `deliverableTimings` + `deliverableDueDate` from `packages/domain`): the studio's own type for that name, else the usual days, counted from the wedding day, the last shoot or today. A date already set is never moved; an unknown anchor (no wedding day yet) stays undated.

- **The Overview says what the client did** (`ClientActivityCard`, 0235): up to three lines, newest first -- "Accepted by Priya · 2 Oct, 7:30 pm", "Quotation opened · 2 Oct, 6:40 pm (3 times)" -- hidden until there is something. The public quotation, invoice and terms reads call `noteClientView` after their answer (never before it, never able to break it); a refresh inside 10 minutes is one visit, and a signed-in member of the studio opening its own link is not counted. "See it as the client" never reaches the public read.

- **The client fills their own details** (0244). More → **Send details form** (and the Wishes tab's amber "Ask Priya to fill them", and the wizard's **Send them a form**) makes a link (`/details/<token>`, `DetailsLinkDialog`) the studio sends on WhatsApp; Settings → Messages → **Client details form** is the studio's one link and QR, and each form sent in makes a client and "Priya & Rahul's Wedding" with its days. The client's words fill only blanks, never a date the studio typed, and once a project has days the client's other events wait as **From the client** chips on the Shoots tab, one tap each. At a plan's limit the client and the dates are still saved and the bell says why.

- **The client never sees unchecked work as ready.** On the client's page a deliverable in review is "ready" (with its link) only once the studio approved or sent it -- Approved, With client, Client approved (`client_portal_status(status, code)`, 0251); With manager reads "in progress". The hand-in's notes are the editor's words to the manager and never reach the client's delivery page.

- **The client's link is a tracker** (`/p/<token>`, 0252): Booked → Shoot days → Footage safe (only for studios that keep data records) → Editing → Ready to view → Delivered, each with one line (`trackerSteps` in `features/client-portal/format.ts`); each photo or film reads Not started · Being edited · Final checks · Ready to view · Delivered, "Expected by" only while the date is ahead (then "Coming soon", never late); the last five updates; one money card (total, paid, balance, "Next: ₹X by date"); Call / WhatsApp / Email the studio. "Looks great" marks the item Client approved. The studio keeps one live link (`client_portal_link_tokens`, service-only) so Share with client copies it again instead of making a new one, and **See it as the client** (`/projects/$id/client-view`) reads it without counting a visit. Never on it: crew pay, staff phones, internal or voice notes, hand-in notes, disk names, expenses, margin.

- **A reply to a note goes to whoever sent it** (0253): the editor's note reaches the last person who wrote to them on that deliverable, else whoever gave the work, else the project's creator; the editor's own alert opens `/my-work?d=`. My work shows each edit once, its 🎤 and note counts open the notes, no figure tiles, filters behind **Filter**, dates as "2 days late".

- **A project has one editor** (`/projects/$id/edit`): the page's Edit and the list's Edit project open it; status stays a one-press select on the page.

- **Shoots are in Google Calendar by a link, never a file per booking** (0257). **Add to Google Calendar** on My shoots gives a person their own bookings; **Shoots in Google Calendar** on the studio's Shoots page gives those who plan (projects edit; the feed checks owner/admin/manager) every shoot with its crew (`features/booking/CalendarLinkButton.tsx`, `GET /calendar/link?scope=`, `POST /calendar/link/rotate`). The link is `/public/calendar/<token>.ics` (`modules/calendar/router.ts`, `buildIcsFeed` in `packages/domain`), read by Google every few hours; a uid per booking or shoot keeps a moved one in place. It carries the shoot, project, venue, map, role and client name -- never pay or a phone, because anyone holding the link reads it -- and **Make a new link** ends the old one at once; a wrong or old token is a plain 404. Tokens live in `calendar_feed_tokens`, service-only.

- **A deliverable carries a note for the team** (owner, 10 Nov: "if any different conversation has been done"). **Note for your team** (`deliverables.internal_notes`) in Add/Edit deliverable and each Create project row ("Anything agreed differently? e.g. Album 40 sheets instead of 30, agreed on call 12 Nov"); the card and My work show an amber sticky-note chip whose hover or tap shows it whole (`features/projects/TeamNoteChip.tsx`), and the drawer edits it. The editor on it writes it too (`PATCH /projects/deliverables/:did/note`, the notes' rule). It is never on the quotation, the client's link or anything the client sees.

- **A booked person can carry a note, like a comment on a cell** (owner, 10 Nov: "in the Google Sheet ... we would use the comment option, visible on hovering"; 0299). `team_assignment_slots.note` / `note_by` / `note_at`, written by whoever plans crew (`POST /allocation/:id/note`), read by everyone who sees the booking, the person included -- it is not money (`cost_notes` stays the payout's own note). Drawn by `features/booking/SlotNote.tsx`: a small amber triangle in the corner of the person's chip with the note, who and when on hover or tap (Team Booking cards, the project's Shoots tab, the month grid's cells), **Add note / Edit note** in every booked row's menu (`useSlotActions`), inline in Assign team, a line on the person's day card and on My shoots, and the count in the calendar's shoot title.

- **Upload a quotation, and the project makes itself** (owner, 18 Nov; 0311). On Projects, **Upload quotation** beside New project (`features/projects/QuotationUpload.tsx`) takes the studio's own quotation, a PDF or a photo; Claude reads it (`lib/quotation-reader.ts`, the Diamond check's engine and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, a JSON schema, never inventing a blank) and the project is made **at once, no review screen**: client, project, each event with its date, hours, city and team as requirements, each deliverable dated from the quotation's own delivery times ("Reels 2 weeks" = 14 days after the last event, a range takes the longer end; untimed ones take the studio's type, else the usual), priced add-ons as extras -- through the same calls Create project makes (`readingToDraft` in `quotation-import.ts`). Then `POST /projects/:id/quotation-tasks` (`planQuotationTasks` in `packages/domain/src/quote-tasks.ts`) gives the team their tasks **by role, no choice on screen**, for **every** deliverable (owner, 18 Nov: "photos, videos, save-the-dates, and everything"; `quoteWorkOf` / `WORK_ROLE`): films and reels to the **video editor**, photos and the gallery to the **photo editor**, albums and frames to the **album designer**, e-invites, save-the-dates and countdowns to the **designer**; video editors who split the work are read from their job role (`videoCutOf`, `jobCoversCut`: full-length films, reels, or short films -- highlights and teasers); no album designer means the photo editor (job roles in Team → Roles; the least busy one in the role; a role nobody holds leaves the task with nobody on it, said on screen), each on the day work must start (promised date less its working days, never before the day after the last event; a design starts today), a **data copy** to the admin the day before anything made from the footage or photos, the **e-invite form** to the manager today when an e-invite is a deliverable, and the **event timeline form** to the manager 8 days before the wedding; each manager task carries the studio's own WhatsApp message and Google Form links (`companies.quote_import_forms`). **One queue per maker across every project** (owner, 18 Nov: "a quotation uploaded 7 days later whose wedding is 20 December, before one for the 28th: their deliverable goes first"; `planQueue`, `bestQueueFor` in `packages/domain/src/quote-schedule.ts`): an editor does one piece at a time, each planned as late as its promise allows, then pushed so the earlier promise goes first whichever was uploaded first; work that holds its maker for days queues (films, reels, photo edits, albums -- `isQueuedWork`), quick jobs (unedited photos, gallery, frames) and designs do not; a new piece goes to whoever in the role can finish it on time with least delay to their other work; earlier quotation tasks still to do are re-dated (their data copy with them) and listed on the upload's screen with earlier/later, and work that cannot make its promise is marked urgent and "N days late". Data copies are one per piece, tied to its deliverable. Work given by hand (Give work) counts as started. Tasks are tagged "From quotation" and given once per project. The editor rides on the task, never on the deliverable (that would read "Editing" months early). **Live for one studio first**: `companies.quote_import`, on only for the studio of the owner's test login (afreenekram98@gmail.com); no "try new features" switch anywhere -- the button simply shows where it is on. 0311 also gives that studio's people their job roles by first name (Sorav admin, Mohit photo editor, Usman and Aman teasers, films and reels, Rayeen full-length films and reels, Sudhansu project manager, Sorav and Mohit e-invites, save-the-dates and countdowns).
