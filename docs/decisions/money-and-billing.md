# Invoices, payments, payouts, expenses and money pages

Owner decisions for this area, moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md on 21 Nov 2026.
They carry the same weight as CLAUDE.md: read this file before you change anything in this area,
and when the owner gives a new standing instruction here, add it below and its title to the index in CLAUDE.md.

- **A client agrees to terms by signing.** The public terms page (`routes/terms-acknowledge.tsx`) asks for a finger signature (`shared/ui/signature-pad.tsx`) before "I agree"; the signature shows on the client's copy and the studio's (`TermsDocumentSheet`).

- **A payout is picked, not typed** (owner, 11 Nov: "I should be able to just click on it ... I should not be using any keyboard"). Assign team's payout is a button (`PayoutButton`, amber "₹ Add payout" while empty, green once set) that opens amounts in place (`PayoutPanel`, `features/shoots/PayoutPicker.tsx`; never a popover inside the dialog): **Their rates**, **Paid <name> before** (last three), **Usually for <role>** (most paid, last 12 months), round amounts ₹1,000-25,000, − / + ₹500 and **Other amount** (`payoutChoices` in `payout-choices.ts`, each amount once). The same panel is in Edit payout and Bulk assign's "Payout for each ticked day". `GET /allocation/payout-memory` (`lib/payout-memory.ts`) feeds it, empty for anyone who cannot see crew pay. **The first payout booked for someone with no usual rate becomes their day rate** (`rememberRates` in `modules/allocation/router.ts`: only for whoever may set pay, never over a saved rate, never for a salaried person; `rates_saved` → "Saved ₹5,000 as Asha's usual rate. Next time it fills itself.").

- **Crew money is owed once the shoot day has passed.** Team payouts opens on **Owed now · Upcoming · All**: Owed now is shoots before India's today minus what is paid; money paid before a shoot is "₹X paid in advance" and never taken off what is owed for shoots done; a released or cancelled booking shows only if money went out. One query (`services/api/src/lib/payout-rows.ts`, `summarizeCrewOwed` in `packages/domain`) feeds the page, the dashboard's **Who you owe** ("₹12,000 to 2 people for shoots already done · Pay"), a member's Pay tab (**Shoot money**) and My payouts, so the numbers never disagree. It reads as the service with the caller's company, because the ledger's RLS hides payments from delegates.

- **One period for the money pages.** Payments received, Expenses, Profit & Loss and Reports share one switch (`features/financials/PeriodSwitch.tsx` + `usePeriod`): This month · Last month · This/Last quarter · This/Last financial year · All time · Custom, in the address as `?period=` (`from`/`to` for Custom) and remembered per studio, so a pick on one page holds on the next; it starts on This month. Invoices keep their own dates (a list for chasing money must not hide last month's unpaid ones), and "now" screens (Shoots, Production board, Attendance, Payroll, Team payouts' Owed now) never take it. A new money page uses `usePeriod`, not its own pills.

- **Payments received opens on what is due.** Four tiles: **Overdue · Due in 30 days · Later · Received** (the period's money in). The first three come from `GET /billing/due`: per project, open invoices, then promises not tied to an invoice, then the rest of the payment plan by its dates (`planDueDate`), anything past the plan Later -- each rupee once (`allocateDue` in `packages/domain/src/payment-plan.ts`, shared with the Billing tab). Tapping a tile lists its lines with the one action: Open invoice, Mark received (date today), or Open billing.

- **A lead's quote is on the studio's letterhead.** The quote viewer and the client's link (`/quote/accept`) print `QuotePaper` (`features/crm/QuotePaper.tsx`) under the same `QuotationLetterhead` as the project quotation: stripe, logo, contacts, number, issued and valid-till. `GET /public/quote/:token` carries `letterhead` beside the quote.

- **The client can sign in front of you.** On the Terms tab, terms that wait carry **Sign now with Priya** (`features/terms/SignHereDialog.tsx`): the terms, the signer's name and the finger signature pad on the studio's phone or tablet, then I agree. `POST /terms/documents/:id/sign-here` goes through the same live link and the same `recordAgreement()` as the client's own page (agreement, signature, bell, owner email), marked "signed in person"; a browser can never claim that mark. The share dialog after sending says "Priya with you? Let them sign here".

- **Recording money received is one form** (`features/billing/RecordPaymentDialog.tsx`): a project's Billing tab, an invoice, + New → Payment received and Payments → Add/Edit all open it (`target`: project, invoice, or pick -- Client then Project on top). Every path asks the reference (UTR / cheque), and a change can move a payment to another of the client's invoices or off one (`PATCH /billing/payments/:id` takes `invoice_id` and a null `project_id`; both invoices' totals follow by trigger, 0145; money on neither is refused) -- the amount stays when it moves. Never a second payment dialog; its pure parts are `payment-form.ts`.

- **Money records can be changed and undone too** (owner, 26 Oct; 0284). An invoice is editable whatever is paid on it -- lines, client, dates and its **number** -- as long as its total does not drop below the money already received (`update_invoice`, the server says so in words); a cancelled invoice has **Reopen invoice** (`reopen_invoice`, money moved off it at cancelling stays on the project) and never takes a payment until reopened; a sent invoice with no payment has **Move back to draft** (its link stops); Delete only while no payment is on it. Payroll: **Reopen month** while nobody in it is paid (`payroll_reopen`), and **Undo paid** on a person marked paid by mistake (`payroll_unmark_paid`, the salary record follows). An expense paid back has **Not paid back yet**; a crew payout payment has **Undo** in its history (a reversal entry, the ledger keeps both); the studio's tax rates are changed from the expense form ("Change your tax rates…"); vendors can be deleted; a saved terms template is updated in place.

- **Invoices are numbered three ways** (owner, 26 Oct; 0284): Settings → Company profile → **How invoices are numbered**: **Running number** (INV-0042), **By the invoice date** (the prefix and the date, INV-20261026, then INV-20261026-2) or **I type each one** (a blank number is refused). `companies.invoice_number_mode`, `next_invoice_number_on(date)`; the invoice form says which and links there.

- **Who signs the invoice is set on the invoice** (owner, 26 Oct). The invoice page's paper carries a screen-only **Edit signature** beside "Authorised signatory", and the invoice form a **Signature** box: a name, a title and a drawn or uploaded signature (`SignatureDialog`, `PUT /billing/signature`), kept on every invoice design's `layout_json` (`signatory_name/title`, `signature_image`, a small PNG data URL) so every invoice shows it, the old ones too; a new design copies it.
